How a visit works

From booking to beading in four steps

  1. Pick a 20-minute window

    Choose your package and arrival time in the customer portal. Same-day slots open every 20 minutes.

  2. Pull up when we text you

    A “bay ready” text arrives 5 minutes before your slot. Wait in the lounge or leave the keys at the desk.

  3. Follow each stage live

    Your dashboard shows the exact stage — foam, hand wash, dry, interior, final check — with a real finish time.

  4. Drive off, points added

    Pay by card, phone or cash in the bay. Points land in your account before you reach the street.
