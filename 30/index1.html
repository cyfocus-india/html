Why paint dulls

Four things a wash can’t fix — and what we do instead

If your car still looks flat after a good wash, one of these is usually the reason. Each needs a different tool, not harder scrubbing.

◎

Swirl marks

What you see
Spider-web circles around reflections in direct sun, worst on dark paint.
How we fix it
Machine polishing levels the clear coat by a few microns — only after we’ve measured it’s thick enough.
⁘

Iron fallout

What you see
Tiny orange specks on light paint and panels that feel rough, mostly behind the wheels.
How we fix it
An iron-dissolving spray turns purple as it bleeds the particles out — no scrubbing required.
◌

Hard-water spots

What you see
Chalky rings left where tap water or sprinklers dried on hot panels.
How we fix it
A mild mineral remover, then a deionized rinse so new spots can’t form while the car dries.
✺

Sap, tar and bug etch

What you see
Sticky drops under trees and black flecks along the rockers after highway trips.
How we fix it
Steam softens them, a clay bar lifts them, and a light polish removes the etch they leave behind.

Protection compared

Wax, sealant or ceramic?

All three make water bead. The difference is how long they last, what they stand up to, and how much prep they need first.

Comparison of carnauba wax, spray sealant and ceramic coating
  Carnauba wax SiO₂ spray sealant Ceramic coating
Lasts 4–6 weeks 6–8 weeks 3 or 5 years
Water behavior Soft, wide beads Tight beads that roll off Tall, round beads; sheets off at highway speed
Stands up to Light UV fading UV and road film UV, bird-dropping etch, road salt and chemical stains
Prep needed A clean wash Wash and iron decon Full decon and at least a 1-step polish
