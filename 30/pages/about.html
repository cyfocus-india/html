About

Started with two buckets and a borrowed pressure washer

Beadwell began in 2014 as a one-van mobile wash. Today we run three hand-wash bays and a detail studio, with the same rule we had on day one: no brush ever touches your paint.

Our story

Twelve years, one rule

  1. 2014

    One van, one route

    Teo Alvarez washes cars in office parking lots around RiNo, with two buckets and a borrowed pressure washer in the back of a van.

  2. 2017

    The Foundry Street bays

    Two hand-wash bays open in a former tire shop. Priya Nand joins to run bookings and the front desk, and the waiting list disappears.

  3. 2020

    Water reclaim and spot-free rinse

    A filtration loop starts reusing most of our wash water, and a reverse-osmosis system ends water spots for good.

  4. 2022

    The detail studio

    A third wash bay plus a two-bay studio with LED inspection panels and infrared curing lamps for ceramic coatings.

  5. 2025

    Customer portal and points

    Online slots, live stage tracking and Beadwell Points — built from two years of feedback cards left at the desk.

Our standards

Six rules every car gets

These aren’t on a poster in the break room. They’re checked on every shift.

  • ⊘

    No brushes, ever

    Only mitts and microfiber touch paint. Wheel brushes never leave the wheel bucket.

  • ◑

    pH-neutral soaps

    Gentle on the wax, sealant or coating you’ve already paid for.

  • ◍

    Fresh towels per car

    Microfiber is laundered on site and retired after 120 washes.

  • ◎

    Measure before polishing

    No machine touches clear coat until we know how thick it is.

  • ▣

    Photos at handover

    Every studio job is photographed, so you have a record for resale.

  • ◷

    Quoted time is real time

    If a booked wash runs late, your next one is on us.
