Wash prices

Hand-wash packages by vehicle size

Bigger vehicles take more product and more time, so prices step up by size class. Not sure which class you’re in? The examples under each heading are a good guide.

Heavy mud or sand: $25–$60 extra, quoted before we start — common after ski trips and trail weekends.

Roof racks and boxes: washed at no charge. We never remove accessories without asking.

Lifted trucks and duallys: Truck price plus $10 for the extra reach and ladder work.

Pricing questions

Before you book

Not for wash bays. Studio work needs a $50 deposit to hold your date; it comes off your final bill and is refundable up to 48 hours before.

Move or cancel a wash slot free of charge up to 2 hours before. If you’re more than 15 minutes late, we’ll fit you into the next open bay or rebook you at no cost.

Only if we find something photos couldn’t show, such as thin clear coat or an old respray. We stop, show you, and re-quote before doing any extra work. You can always decline.

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay and cash. Wash Club renews on the same card each month, and you can update it in your dashboard.
