Booking & payment

Three payments, clear policies

  1. 30%

    To block your date

    Paid when you sign the agreement. Your crew and date are reserved from this moment.

  2. 50%

    A week before

    Due seven days before your first event, after the final timeline call.

  3. 20%

    On final delivery

    Paid when your full gallery and films are handed over. UPI, bank transfer or card.

Postponement

Move your date once at no charge, as long as the new date is free on our calendar.

Cancellation

The retainer isn't refundable, but we hold it as credit towards any shoot for 12 months.

Destination travel

Rajasthan: flat ₹18,000. Rest of India: ₹45,000 plus crew stay. Abroad: flights and stay at cost.

Taxes & invoices

All prices exclude 18% GST. You'll receive a GST invoice for every payment you make.
