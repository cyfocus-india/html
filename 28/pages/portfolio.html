Colour styles

See one frame in our three edits

You choose a look per event — or leave it to us to decide frame by frame. Switch between them below.

Warm Film

Soft golden highlights and gentle grain, inspired by Kodak Portra. Our most requested look for day events and pre-wedding shoots.

True Colour

Exactly what the eye saw: accurate lehenga reds and natural skin. Ideal for couples who want outfits remembered precisely.

Timeless Mono

Rich, high-contrast black and white for emotional moments — the vidaai, the speeches, the grandparents. Usually 10–15% of a gallery.
