Candid photography

We don't pose you — we position ourselves

Candid coverage means reading the room a second before something happens. Here is how we do it at every event.

  • ◈

    Two angles, always

    One photographer stays with the couple while the second works the crowd, so reactions are captured alongside the moment itself.

  • ◌

    Silent during rituals

    Electronic shutters and no flash during pheras, nikah or anand karaj. The pandit or granthi never has to pause for us.

  • ❖

    Your must-have list

    Name up to 25 people who matter most. We track them across every event and quietly fill any gaps before the night ends.

  • ☀︎

    Light we add, light we don't

    Bounced, off-camera light only after sunset — never a direct flash in someone's face during a tearful moment.

₹75,000 per event day for standalone candid coverage

Drone & aerial

The whole celebration, from 120 metres up

Aerial footage shows what no ground camera can: the scale of a baraat, a palace lit for the sangeet, a lake at dawn. Every flight is planned, permitted and insured.

  • ✔︎

    Licensed and registered

    Flown by a DGCA-certified remote pilot with drones registered on the Digital Sky platform. Restricted-zone permissions are filed 10 days ahead.

  • △

    Safety before spectacle

    No flights directly over guests, a wind cut-off of 35 km/h and a spotter on the ground for every take.

  • ◉

    Indoor FPV on request

    A small, guarded FPV drone can glide through a sangeet hall or haveli courtyard in one continuous shot.

₹35,000 per event day · included in Heirloom
