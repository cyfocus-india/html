Frequently asked questions about workshop pricing

What happens if additional worn parts are found during service?

We strictly never fit new parts or bill extra work without your written approval. Our mechanic photographs the worn component and sends a clear WhatsApp estimate with options before any replacement happens.

Can I bring my own engine oil, filters, or brake pads?

Yes. If you prefer a specific brand of sealed, manufacturer-grade engine oil or performance brake pads, we gladly perform labour-only installation charged at our standard hourly rates without any extra markup.

Are workshop cleaning consumables included in package rates?

Yes. Solvent cleaners, degreasers, copper crush washers, and high-cling chain wax used during standard servicing are already bundled into our tiered rates—no surprise “miscellaneous workshop supplies” line items.

What warranty is included with workshop repairs?

Eligible mechanical and maintenance work carries our 90-day Ironlane Workmanship Assurance. If an adjusted or repaired component requires re-inspection within 90 days, we check and adjust it free of charge.
