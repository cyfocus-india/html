Privacy policy.

How this demonstration template treats information in its current static form.

1. Current demo behaviour

The appointment, sign-in and sign-up forms validate information in your browser. In this unconnected demo, submissions are not transmitted to Ironlane Moto Works or stored by this website.

2. Local preferences

The site uses local browser storage to remember color-theme and text-direction preferences. These values remain on the device and do not identify you.

3. Embedded services

The contact page embeds a map from OpenStreetMap. Loading that frame may share ordinary technical request information, such as an IP address, with the map provider under its own policies.

4. Production forms

When you connect a form endpoint, state what data is collected, the business purpose, the legal basis, the retention period, who receives it and how a person may exercise their rights.

5. Security

Use encrypted transport, least-privilege access, secure form processing and an appropriate retention schedule. Never collect payment or identity information through an unprotected static form.

6. Contact

For this demonstration, contact hello@ironlanemoto.in. Replace this address with the verified privacy contact before launch.
