Workshop Standards

What Good Care Looks Like in the Bay.

We replace workshop guesswork with measured proof, visual inspection, and clear rider consent before turning a spanner.

Standard 01

Diagnostic Transparency

Technical scanner accuracy and multimeter measurements explained in plain language without assuming you have garage vocabulary.

Standard 02

Evidence-First Inspection

Standard 03

Pre-Approval & Handover

Zero surprise additions. You receive itemized estimates first, photo evidence of worn parts, and a written checklist at vehicle handover.

Work in focus

The detail behind a better ride.

Illustrative workshop cases show how we approach a job, from the rider’s concern to the final checks. These are sample scenarios, not verified customer projects.

A smoother daily commute

Concern: Uneven drive and noise at low speed.

Work: Inspect chain and sprocket wear, set slack and alignment, then clean and lubricate.

Final check: Confirm free movement and consistent drive on the road check.

Consistent brake feel

Concern: A lever that feels different from one ride to the next.

Work: Check pad and disc condition, caliper movement and fluid before agreeing repairs.

Final check: Confirm lever response, operation and the absence of leaks.

Finding a running fault

Concern: Intermittent hesitation or an engine warning light.

Work: Review fault data and carry out targeted mechanical and electrical checks.

Final check: Verify the repair against the original symptoms and record the findings.
