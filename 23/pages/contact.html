Getting to 12th Main

  • By metro

    Indiranagar station on the Purple Line is a 12-minute walk. Head along 100 Feet Road and turn onto 12th Main.

  • By road

    Two-wheeler parking at the entrance. Cars use paid street parking on 12th Main, which fills up after 6 pm.

  • Access

    Step-free entrance and a lift to the first floor. A wheelchair is available at reception.
