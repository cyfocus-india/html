Our own label

Lumora Atelier: frames we design and make in small batches

We started Atelier in 2019 when customers kept asking for designer-quality acetate without the designer price. Each shape is drawn in our Indiranagar studio, cut from Italian cellulose acetate and hand-polished by a family workshop in Mumbai.

  • Five-barrel hinges and wire-cored arms you can adjust for years
  • 14 shapes in three colours each, sized from 47 to 55 mm
  • About 40% below comparable designer acetate — no distributor margin
  • Two-year warranty against manufacturing faults

Authorised stockist

Every branded frame is genuine and traceable

  1. Official distributors only

    We buy from each brand’s authorised Indian distributor — never grey-market imports or “first copies”.

  2. Complete in the box

    The original case, cleaning cloth and warranty card come with every branded frame.

  3. Serial number on your invoice

    Your GST invoice lists model, colour and serial number for warranty or insurance claims.

  4. We handle warranty claims

    If a hinge or coating fails within the brand’s warranty, we deal with the distributor so you don’t have to.

Can’t see the model you want? We’ll order it in

Send us the brand and model number. If the distributor has it, it arrives in 5–7 working days with a refundable ₹1,000 deposit.
