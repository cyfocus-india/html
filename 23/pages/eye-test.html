What happens in the room

Your comprehensive exam, step by step

A standard test covers steps 1, 2, 3 and 6. Nothing touches your eye, and you can ask to pause at any point.

  1. 1

    History & lifestyle

    Medicines, health conditions, screen hours and whatever is bothering you.

    5 min
  2. 2

    Auto-refraction

    A machine estimates your power in seconds as a starting point.

    3 min
  3. 3

    Refraction

    “Better with one or two?” The optometrist fine-tunes your prescription on the phoropter.

    10 min
  4. 4

    Eye pressure

    A gentle puff of air measures the pressure inside the eye — the key glaucoma check.

    2 min
  5. 5

    Retina & OCT scan

    A slit-lamp view and a 3D scan of the retina, saved for comparison at future visits.

    12 min
  6. 6

    Results explained

    Your prescription and scan images on screen, in plain language, with time for questions.

    8 min

Before you come

  • Bring your current glasses and any old prescriptions.
  • Contact lens wearers: wear your lenses in and bring the box or foil showing the power.
  • Write down any medicines you take, including eye drops.
  • If your eyes might be dilated, bring sunglasses and plan not to drive for four hours.
  • Arrive ten minutes early and take the lift to the first floor.

Questions before your visit? Call the clinic desk on +91 80 4127 6650 (ext. 1).

Eye test questions

Good to know before you book

Is the ₹350 test fee really refunded?

Yes. Buy a complete pair — frame plus prescription lenses — within 30 days and the fee comes off your bill automatically. It also applies to the Children’s Eye Test.

Is an optometrist the same as an eye doctor?

Optometrists test vision and screen eye health. If we see signs of disease such as cataract or glaucoma, we refer you to a partner ophthalmologist in Indiranagar within the week and send our scans ahead.

Can I get tested without buying glasses here?

Of course. The prescription is yours: we email a PDF within 24 hours and you can fill it anywhere.

Can you test a child who can’t read yet?

Yes, from age four we use picture charts and matching games. For children under four, we refer to a paediatric ophthalmologist.

Do you accept insurance or corporate health plans?

We don’t bill insurers directly, but we give an itemised GST invoice and a signed prescription, which most corporate health plans accept for reimbursement.

How do I reschedule or cancel?

Use the link in your SMS confirmation or call us. Changes are free up to two hours before your slot.

After your test, choose frames the same visit

Your new prescription goes straight to our opticians, who’ll shortlist frames that suit both your face and your lens power.
