Frame size guide

Read the three numbers inside your current frame

Look on the inside of a temple arm. A frame marked 52□18-140 has:

  • 52 mm lens width — the most important number for fit
  • 18 mm bridge — the gap between the lenses, over your nose
  • 140 mm temple — arm length from hinge to the tip behind your ear
