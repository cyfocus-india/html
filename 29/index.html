How a class night works

Three hours, from apron to supper

Never taken a cooking class? Here's exactly what happens on an evening class at Copper Pot.

  1. 1
    6:15 PM

    Doors open, aprons on

    Pick up a drink at the bar, find your name card and meet the person sharing your bench.

  2. 2
    6:30 PM

    The chef's demo

    A 20-minute walkthrough of the night's techniques, with a camera over the stove so everyone can see into the pan.

  3. 3
    6:50 PM

    Cook at your station

    Work through the recipes at your bench. The chef circulates, tastes your sauce and adjusts your knife grip as you go.

  4. 4
    8:45 PM

    Sit down at the long table

    Plates go to our 24-seat table and everyone eats what they cooked. Leftovers go home in compostable boxes.

  5. 5
    Next morning

    Recipes land in your dashboard

    Every recipe card, plus the chef's notes from your class, unlocks in your student dashboard to keep.
