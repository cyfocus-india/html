A workshop weekend

Hour by hour

This is how the Pasta Fresca Intensive runs. Every workshop follows the same rhythm: foundations on Saturday, independence on Sunday.

  1. 9:45 AM

    Coffee and pastries

    Meet your bench partner and the chef, and set up your station.

  2. 10:00 AM

    Foundations demo

    Flours, hydration, and why yolk-heavy dough behaves differently.

  3. 10:45 AM

    Dough lab

    Mix, knead and rest three doughs side by side to feel the difference.

  4. 12:30 PM

    Lunch at the long table

    A chef-cooked lunch with an optional glass of wine.

  5. 1:30 PM

    Shaping session

    Tagliatelle, pappardelle and orecchiette, by hand and by machine.

  6. 3:15 PM

    Sauce bar

    Ragù bianco and burro e salvia, matched to the shapes you made.

  1. 9:45 AM

    Coffee and recap

    Review Saturday's notes and questions over coffee.

  2. 10:00 AM

    Filled pasta

    Tortellini, ravioli and agnolotti, with three fillings.

  3. 12:00 PM

    Lunch from your doughs

    We cook and eat the pasta you shaped on Saturday.

  4. 1:00 PM

    Solo challenge

    You plan and cook one plate with no recipe, just what you've learned.

  5. 3:00 PM

    Chef tasting

    One-to-one feedback on your plate and your technique.

  6. 3:45 PM

    Pack up and certificate

    Collect your kit, workbook and signed certificate.

Workshop or class?

How a workshop compares to an evening class

Both are fully hands-on. The difference is depth, and how much time you get with the chef.

Evening class compared with weekend workshop
What you getEvening classWeekend workshop
Cooking time3 hours, one evening12 hours over two days
Group sizeUp to 12 studentsUp to 10 students
Price$75–$165 per seat$295–$395 per person
FoodDinner of the dishes you cookLunch both days, plus what you cook
Chef feedbackTips at your stationOne-to-one tasting and review
Best forTrying a new cuisineMastering one skill properly
What you take home

More than leftovers

Every workshop ticket includes a kit chosen for that skill. These are tools you'll actually use again, not souvenirs.

✂

Skill kit

A pasta cutter and board, a banneton and lame, or a granite mortar, depending on the workshop.

✎

Printed workbook

Splash-proof, spiral-bound notes with every recipe, ratio and troubleshooting chart.

★

Signed certificate

A certificate of completion signed by your chef. Frame-worthy, we're told.

☏

Follow-up video call

15 minutes with your chef within 30 days to troubleshoot what you cook at home.
