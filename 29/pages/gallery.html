The space

Three rooms and one long table

A converted 1920s bakery on NW Glisan Street, restored with plenty of daylight, strong ventilation and a lot of counter space.

  • Demo Kitchen

    12 stations

    Our main classroom: twelve induction stations around a demo island with an overhead camera.

  • Pastry Room

    10 benches

    Cool marble benches, two deck ovens and proofing cabinets. Home of the weekend workshops.

  • Pantry & Shop

    Open daily

    Our ingredient store, plus the shelf of knives and pans we teach with.

  • The Long Table

    Seats 24

    One reclaimed-fir table where every class ends with dinner together.

Plan your visit

Designed so every cook can join in

Tell us what you need when you book and it will be ready before you arrive.

♿

Step-free entry

Level entrance from the street, wide doorways and an accessible restroom.

⇕

Adjustable stations

Two stations lower to seated height. Let us know and we'll reserve one for you.

◉

Clear demos

The overhead camera feeds two large screens, and chefs wear a mic linked to our hearing loop.

⌂

Stools at every station

Prefer to cook sitting down for part of the class? There's a stool waiting at every bench.

▣

Lockers and coat rack

Free lockers for bags and coats, so your station stays clear and safe.

❀

A quiet corner

A calm nook off the long table if you need a break from the buzz.
