Skill levels

Which level is right for you?

Every class is graded by technique, not by how fancy the food sounds. Here's how to pick.

Beginner

You're ready if you can boil pasta and want to feel calm and confident in your own kitchen.

You'll learn

  • Holding and using a chef's knife safely
  • Seasoning by taste, not by teaspoon
  • Timing three dishes to finish together

Start with Knife Skills Bootcamp

Intermediate

You're ready if you cook a few nights a week and can follow a recipe without stress.

You'll learn

  • Making doughs and fillings by feel
  • Controlling wok and pan heat
  • Building sauces from the fond

Start with Handmade Ravioli

Advanced

You're ready if knife work is second nature and you want projects that reward patience.

You'll learn

  • Laminating butter into dough
  • Long-simmered broths and pastes
  • Precise pastry temperatures

Start with Ramen from the Broth Up

Good to know

Before you book

Quick answers about dietary needs, cancellations and what to bring.

Still unsure? Message the studio and a chef will point you to the class that suits you.

Ask us directly →
I have a food allergy. Can I still join?

Yes. Add it to your booking at least 48 hours ahead and we'll prepare swapped ingredients for your station. Our kitchen handles nuts, gluten and shellfish, so we can't promise a trace-free space for severe allergies.

What's your cancellation policy?

Change or cancel for free up to 72 hours before class and we'll return the full amount as class credit. Inside 72 hours you can send a friend in your place at no cost.

What should I wear and bring?

Closed-toe shoes and something to tie back long hair. We provide aprons, knives, all equipment and boxes for leftovers.

Can I come on my own?

Of course. About a third of our students come solo. You'll share a bench with another cook and usually leave with a new friend.

Is there a minimum age?

Evening classes are 18+ because wine is served. Teens aged 12–17 can join Saturday morning classes with an adult who also has a seat booked.

Can I buy the equipment we use?

Yes. Our shop shelf stocks the same chef's knives, carbon-steel woks and Dutch ovens we teach with, and students get 10% off on class night.

Not sure where to start?

Start with Knife Skills Bootcamp

It's our most-booked first class for a reason: two and a half hours, $75, and every recipe you cook afterwards gets faster.
