Our Story & Purpose

Democratizing Clean Energy for Every Indian Rooftop

Founded by renewable energy engineers and grid specialists, SolarNest was born out of a simple realization: while solar technology has reached unprecedented efficiency, the homeowner experience remained complicated, opaque, and intimidating.

We set out to change that. By combining advanced 3D rooftop simulation, certified Bloomberg Tier-1 photovoltaic components, and turnkey DISCOM net-metering navigation, we turn complex technical jargon into predictable monthly savings.

Our mission is to help 100,000+ Indian households achieve true energy independence by 2030, contributing directly to India's carbon-neutral grid goals while shielding families from escalating utility tariffs.

What We Do

End-to-End Solar Services ●

From drone survey and shadow analysis to commissioning and lifetime app monitoring, we handle every detail.

1. 3D Drone & Shadow Analysis

We use high-precision LiDAR mapping and 365-day solar path modeling to identify optimal panel azimuth, roof tilt, and avoid chimney/tree shade.

Precision Layout

2. Tier-1 Hardware Procurement

Strictly Bloomberg Tier-1 TOPCon and Mono PERC bifacial modules paired with smart IP65 string or hybrid inverters built for Indian grid voltages.

Top Tier Equipment

3. DISCOM Net-Metering

Full liaisoning and technical documentation with BESCOM, MSEDCL, TSSPDCL, TANGEDCO, and other state utilities for seamless bidirectional net-meter swaps.

Guaranteed Approvals

4. PM Surya Ghar Subsidy Direct Credit

100% digital submission on the National Rooftop Solar Portal. We prepare single-line diagrams, inspection paperwork, and verify direct bank transfer (DBT).

Zero Paperwork Stress

Engineered anodized aluminum structures rated for 150 km/h wind gusts with non-penetrating seam clamps for clay, tile, or RCC roofs with zero drilling leaks.

100% Leak-Proof Mount

Live smartphone generation telemetry with automated cloud fault detection. Includes periodic panel cleaning, string diagnostics, and preventive safety audits.
