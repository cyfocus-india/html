Indian Solar Standards & Calculation Assumptions

Daily Solar Generation: Sized at ~4.2 kWh/day per kWp (approx. 126 units/kW/month in Indian sunlight conditions).
Net Metering Offset: Offsets up to 95% of energy charges. Remaining ~5% accounts for DISCOM fixed meter load tariffs.
Surplus Grid Export: Excess generation fed back into grid is credited at state DISCOM solar rate (~₹3.50/unit APPC).
PM Surya Ghar Subsidy: Central subsidy of ₹30,000 (1 kW), ₹60,000 (2 kW), and ₹78,000 (3–10 kW) is factored into net payback.
