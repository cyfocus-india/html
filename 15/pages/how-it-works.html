DISCOM Approvals & Net-Metering Roadmap ●

Dealing with state power utilities, electrical safety inspectorates, and national portals can feel daunting. SolarNest manages 100% of the liaison on your behalf.

Day 1 – 3

National Portal Filing

We submit your consumer number, KYC, and roof site dimensions onto the central pmsuryaghar.gov.in portal.

Application Ref Generated
Day 4 – 10

DISCOM Feasibility Sanction

Local utility verifies your local distribution transformer (DT) capacity to ensure seamless two-way power flow.

Technical Sanction Issued
Day 11 – 15

CEIG Safety Clearance

Chief Electrical Inspectorate verifies physical installation safety, earth pit ohm values (<2Ω), and dual SPD surge protection.

Safety Compliance Passed
Day 16 – 21

Bi-Directional Meter Swap

Utility engineers replace old unidirectional meter with certified smart bidirectional meter. Grid synchronization complete!
