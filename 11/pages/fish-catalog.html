SPECIES QUESTIONS

A few things worth knowing.

Can I buy directly from this website?

This local store concept lets you browse and prepare a shortlist. It does not take payments, reserve livestock, or place an order.

Are the fish photographs of current stock?

The photographs are original generated illustrations of the collection. They do not show actual inventory or guarantee exact coloration.

What does “planted tank” mean in the filters?

It highlights freshwater species to consider for a planted setup. It is a starting point for research, not a guarantee that every fish, plant, and companion will be compatible.
