FILTRATION ARCHITECTURE

The lungs of the tank.
Choosing the right circulation style.

Filtration isn't just about moving water; it provides the surface area for beneficial nitrifying bacteria to thrive and stabilizes your water clarity.

Canister · External

External Canister Systems

Highest biological media capacity, ultra-silent operation, and minimal tank clutter. Elegant glass lily pipes keep the display pristine in aquascapes and medium-to-large community aquariums.

Hang-On-Back · Rim

Hang-On-Back (HOB) Units

Slim, space-saving design resting on the tank rim. Provides gentle waterfall oxygenation, effortless 30-second filter cartridge maintenance, and reliable mechanical polish for desktop nanos.

Sponge · Air-Driven

Bio-Sponge Filters

Zero mechanical suction hazard, supreme aeration, and dense biological colonization. The uncontested choice for shrimp breeding, nano fries, quarantine setups, and hospital tanks.

BEFORE YOU SWITCH IT ON

Common equipment questions.

Can one filter suit every aquarium?

No. Aquarium volume is only part of the decision. Livestock, flow preferences, and the maintenance plan matter too.

Do I need an aquarium heater?

It depends on the species and room conditions. Research the required water temperature and choose suitable, safe equipment.

Is a tank kit all I need?

Check the contents carefully. Water testing, substrate, a safe stand, food, and other care supplies may need to be purchased separately.
