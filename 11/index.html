Step-by-Step Aquarium Setup Guide

Follow our four fundamental phases to build a balanced, crystal clear aquarium that thrives for years.

Read complete guide

Step 1: Placement & Hardscape Layout

Place your rimless tank on a level, foam-padded stand away from direct sunlight. Arrange natural dragon stone, seiryu rock, and spider driftwood to create swimming channels, caves, and visual depth.

Step 2: Aqua Soil Substrate & Live Planting

Slope nutrient-rich volcanic aqua soil from front (3cm) to back (7cm). Moisten the soil, then use stainless steel tweezers to insert carpeting plants (Monte Carlo) and midground Anubias and crypts.

Step 3: Gentle Filling & The 3-Week Nitrogen Cycle

Lay bubble wrap on substrate and slowly fill with dechlorinated water. Power on your filter and heater (25°C). Dose beneficial nitrifying bacteria daily until Ammonia and Nitrite drop to 0 ppm.

Step 4: Acclimation & Adding Livestock

Float fish bags for 20 minutes to equalize water temperature. Gradually drip-acclimate tank water over 40 minutes, then gently net your fish into their new underwater home. Keep lights dim for 12 hours.
