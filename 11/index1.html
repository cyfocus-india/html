A THOUGHT BEFORE YOU DIVE IN

Big wonder. Better questions.

Is a marine aquarium right for my first tank?

It can be rewarding, but it requires dedicated equipment, careful water preparation, and consistent maintenance. Start with a realistic budget and a complete species-specific plan.

Should I choose the fish or the aquarium first?

Research the species first, then plan the space and equipment around their adult needs. Avoid choosing livestock solely because they fit into an empty aquarium today.

Can I mix freshwater and marine species?

No. These are different environments with different water requirements. Keep a clear plan for one compatible environment.

Can you help me find something unusual?

Use the special order enquiry to describe the species and your existing setup. Availability and suitability would need to be confirmed by the store.
