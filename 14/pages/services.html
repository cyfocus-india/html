What Actually Happens on Move Day

The detail that separates a professional relocation from two people and a hired van.

100%Items counted
Step 01 — Inventory

Every Carton Is Counted Before It Moves

Your supervisor walks each room with you and builds a digital manifest before a single box is sealed. Each carton gets a room colour, a number and a fragility grade, and the same list is checked again at delivery.

  • Photographic record of high-value items at pickup
  • Room-wise colour codes that match the new floor plan
  • Digital sign-off sheet shared to your dashboard
Typical duration 30–45 minutes
Who is present Supervisor + you
5Cushion layers
Step 02 — Packing & Loading

Five Layers Between Your Things and the Road

Fragile goods are wrapped in bubble, separated with corrugated sheet, cushioned with foam, boxed in 5-ply cartons and finally blanket-padded inside the vehicle. Heavy appliances travel upright on dollies, strapped to the container wall.

  • Virgin bubble wrap and stretch film, never reused
  • Appliance dollies and tie-down strapping in every vehicle
  • Load plan built so the last box in is the first box out
Materials included No extra charge

A numbered tamper-evident seal goes on in front of you, and the same number is verified before unloading starts. In between, the vehicle reports its position to your dashboard and your move coordinator stays reachable on one number.
