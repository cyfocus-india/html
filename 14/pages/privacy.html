Privacy Policy

How MoveMate collects, handles and protects your personal information across our website, customer dashboard and moving operations.

At MoveMate, we value the trust you place in us when inviting our team into your home or office. This policy describes how we collect, handle and protect your personal information across our website, customer dashboard and moving operations.

1. Information We Collect

To provide accurate moving estimates and coordinate relocations, we collect only necessary details including:

  • Contact information (Full name, mobile number, email address).
  • Relocation specifics (Pickup address, delivery address, floor numbers, elevator access, preferred dates).
  • Inventory details (Home size, approximate item count, fragile/specialty item disclosures).
  • Account credentials and billing transaction records.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for operational moving requirements:

  • Generating instant and survey-confirmed price estimates.
  • Dispatching verified packing teams, container vehicles and move coordinators.
  • Transmitting live GPS route progress and delivery status SMS updates.
  • Issuing official tax invoices and facilitating secure online payments.
  • Improving service reliability and training our customer care specialists.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols for all stored and transmitted information. Your contact details and address logs are never sold, rented, or distributed to third-party telemarketers.

4. Cookies & Local Preferences

Our website utilizes lightweight browser local storage to preserve your theme preference (Dark/Light mode), layout direction (LTR/RTL), and customer dashboard session state.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to inspect, update or request deletion of your personal contact records at any time after the completion of your move.

6. Contact Privacy Team

For any inquiries regarding data protection or our privacy practices, please contact us at privacy@movemate.example or reach our headquarters at MoveMate House, Sector 44, Gurugram, India.
