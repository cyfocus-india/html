Why We Exist

Moving Should Feel Exciting, Never Uncertain

MoveMate was founded in 2016 after one of us paid three times the quoted price on a Bengaluru-to-Pune shift — the extra appearing as "loading charges", "stair charges" and a tip the driver would not leave without. Nothing about that was unusual. It was simply how the industry worked.

So we built the opposite: a fixed written quote before the crew is dispatched, an itemised GST invoice afterwards, and the two numbers matching to the rupee. Everything else we do follows from that one commitment.

  • The quote you accept is the invoice you pay
  • No loading, stair, toll or tipping surcharges, ever
  • Cancel free up to 48 hours before your slot
Our Guiding Philosophy

Four Principles We Do Not Trade Away

These decide what we say yes to — and the business we turn down.

Care First Handling

Every item is packed to a written specification for its category — glassware, electronics, artwork and furniture each have their own protocol, and the crew is audited against it.

One Person Accountable

Each move gets a named coordinator who owns it end to end. You never repeat your address to a new agent, and there is always one number that reaches someone who knows your job.

Fair, Transparent Pricing

Itemised estimates with GST included. If our survey underestimates the job, we absorb the difference rather than renegotiating with a loaded truck in your driveway.

Our Own People

Movers and drivers are salaried employees with background checks, uniforms, insurance and paid training — not daily-wage labour hired at the gate on the morning of your move.

Our Story

From One Truck to Forty Hubs

2016

One truck, one city

Founded in Gurugram with a single leased container truck and a promise that the quote would not change on move day.

2019

Crews brought in-house

We stopped subcontracting entirely and put every mover, packer and driver on payroll — the change that made our damage rate fall by two-thirds.

2022

Live tracking for customers

GPS on every long-distance vehicle, and the customer dashboard that lets you follow checkpoints and pull your own invoice.

2026

1,000+ cities covered

Forty regional hubs, 312 owned vehicles and more than 5,000 households moved with a 98% on-time record.
