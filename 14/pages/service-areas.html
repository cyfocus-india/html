High-Rise & Township Standards

Pre-Approved Protocols for 2,500+ Indian Gated Communities

Moving into a high-rise or gated township in India comes with strict association bylaws. MoveMate handles society permissions, basement vehicle clearances, and service lift protection so you never face gate rejections.

Pillar 01

Basement Low-Clearance Dispatch

Most luxury high-rises ban surface truck parking. Our 14 ft City Sprinters are built with a sub-2.4m chassis that comfortably enters underground basement parking ramps, parking directly at your lift core.

  • Fits under 2.4m basement sprinkler pipes
  • Zero parking obstruction for society residents
  • Eliminates 200m long-distance carry across podiums
Pillar 02

Quilted Lift Armor & Floor Runners

Society management committees charge steep penalties for scratched elevators or damaged marble. We line service elevators with heavy quilted canvas blankets and lay neoprene floor runners in lobbies.

  • Protects elevator mirror & stainless-steel panels
  • Heavy-duty runner mats prevent floor scuffs
  • Corner edge guards for corridor doorway frames
Pillar 03

Digital Gate Pass & App Integration

No waiting at security gates. 24 hours before moving day, MoveMate automatically generates verified driver KYC, crew government IDs, and vehicle insurance documents directly for your society security gate.

  • Direct integration with MyGate, ADDA & NoBrokerHood
  • Police-verified crew Aadhaar KYC pre-uploaded
  • Society move-in NOC verification handled
Pillar 04

Bylaw & Silent-Shift Compliance

Indian residential welfare associations enforce strict shifting windows. MoveMate synchronizes teams to operate strictly within permitted hours, minimizing noise and removing all empty cartons before leaving.

  • 100% adherence to 10 AM – 6 PM shifting bylaws
  • Zero noisy power drilling during restricted quiet hours
  • Complete carton breakdown & corridor sweep-clean
