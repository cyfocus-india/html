Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ExamSuccess protects your personal information and learning preferences.

On this pageInformationLocal storageYour choicesContact

Information we collect

When you register or contact us, we collect necessary account details such as your name, email address, contact phone number, target exam preferences, and login credentials to personalize your preparation journey.

Preferences & local storage

The platform stores your theme (light/dark mode) and layout direction preferences in local storage so your preferred learning interface remains consistent across sessions. No intrusive third-party advertising trackers are used.

Your data choices

You have full control over your saved account preferences and theme settings, which can be modified or cleared at any time through your account or browser settings.

Contact & privacy inquiries

If you have any questions regarding our privacy practices or data handling, please reach out to our student support team through the contact page.
