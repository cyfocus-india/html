Quantitative Aptitude ★★☆ Moderate (SBI PO Level)

A train 240 m long passes a pole in 16 seconds and passes a platform in 36 seconds. What is the length of the platform?

Shortcut Technique:

Speed = 240 m / 16 s = 15 m/s. In 36 s, total distance = 15 × 36 = 540 m. Platform length = 540 - 240 = 300 m (Option B). Shortcut: Ratio 16 : (36 - 16) = 16 : 20 = 4 : 5. Length = (240 / 4) × 5 = 300 m.
