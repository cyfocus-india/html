Packing guide

Pack it once, pack it right

Nine out of ten damaged parcels we see come down to packing. These six habits prevent nearly all of them.

  • Box inside a boxGlass, ceramics and electronics need a double-wall carton with 5 cm of cushioning on every side.
  • Do the shake testClose the box and shake it. If anything moves, add paper or air pillows.
  • H-tape every seamTape the centre seam and both edges, top and bottom. One strip is not enough.
  • One label, flat facePut the label on the largest face and cover every old barcode.
  • Seal the liquidsBottles go in a zip bag with the cap taped, upright and marked "This side up".
  • Mark fragile itemsParcels marked "Fragile" go down our hand-sort lane instead of the conveyor.
