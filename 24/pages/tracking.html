Status guide

What each tracking status means

Eight statuses cover a parcel's whole life with us. Here's what's happening behind each one.

Booked

We have the details and a label number, but no rider has scanned the parcel yet.

Picked up

The rider scanned it at your door. The pickup receipt is in your SMS.

Sorted at hub

Weighed, photographed and assigned to the next bike, van, truck or flight.

In transit

Moving between cities. Scans appear at each hub, roughly every 6 to 12 hours.

Out for delivery

With the rider on today's beat. The receiver gets an OTP and a live ETA link.

Delivered

Handed over with an OTP, signature or doorstep photo. Proof is saved for 12 months.

Delivery attempted

The rider couldn't complete the drop. A second attempt is booked automatically.

Returning to sender

After two failed attempts and 5 days at the hub, the parcel heads back to you.

Something wrong?

Late, missed or damaged: what to do next

⏱

It's running late

  1. Check the time of the latest scan above.
  2. No new scan for 6 hours (local) or 24 hours (outstation)? Message us with the DM number.
  3. We escalate to the hub lead and reply with a firm new ETA within 2 hours.
🚪

Nobody was home

  1. The second attempt is the next working day, 10 AM to 1 PM.
  2. Reply to the SMS to pick another day, redirect to a neighbour, or collect from a counter.
  3. Uncollected parcels wait 5 days at the hub before returning to the sender.
📷

It arrived damaged

  1. Note the damage in the rider's app, or refuse the parcel at the door.
  2. Photograph the outer box and the contents within 48 hours.
  3. Raise a claim from the dashboard or the contact page, with the item invoice.
