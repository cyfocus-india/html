About Dashmile

A Pune courier built by people who've worked the night shift

We started Dashmile in 2017 after ten years of running other companies' sorting hubs, sure that a city courier could be fast and still pick up the phone.

410 people 3 hubs in Pune 4.2 million parcels delivered

Our story

From six scooters to three hubs

  1. 2017

    Six scooters in Kothrud

    Rohan and Meera start with six riders, delivering for bookshops and boutiques around FC Road.

  2. 2019

    The Hadapsar hub opens

    A proper sorting floor lets us promise same-day delivery across the whole city for the first time.

  3. 2021

    Air Express takes off

    Overnight cargo space out of Pune airport connects our customers to six metros by the next day.

  4. 2023

    Electric three-wheelers arrive

    38 EVs replace diesel vans on the busiest city beats, quieter and cheaper to run.

  5. 2025

    PCMC gets its own hub

    The Bhosari hub cuts average delivery time in Pimpri-Chinchwad by 40 minutes.

  6. 2026

    4.2 million parcels

    And the founders still read every escalation that reaches them.

Our network

Three hubs and the vehicles behind them

Each hub has its own job, which is how a parcel can cross Pune in under three hours.

Main sortation hub

Hadapsar

The heart of the network, running 24 hours a day, six days a week.

  • 42,000 sq ft sorting floor
  • 38,000 parcels a day capacity
  • Linehaul trucks leave nightly at 10:30 PM
PCMC hub

Bhosari

Serves Pimpri-Chinchwad and the industrial belt to the north-west.

  • 18,000 sq ft in MIDC Bhosari
  • Feeds Chakan and Talegaon beats
  • Own sorting wave at 7 PM
City counter

Baner

A walk-in counter and the rider base for the west side of the city.

  • Drop-off and packing counter
  • Base for 24 Dash 90 riders
  • Covers Baner, Aundh, Pashan and Balewadi

Fleet on the road today

  • Two-wheelers146
  • Electric three-wheelers38
  • Delivery vans22
  • Linehaul trucks6

Every vehicle carries GPS and a fire extinguisher. Vans are serviced every 5,000 km.

How we work

Four promises we hold ourselves to

🛵

Riders come first

Every rider has accident insurance, a fixed beat and no penalty for slowing down in heavy rain.

⚡

Cleaner kilometres

62% of our city kilometres are electric today. We'll reach 85% by 2028 without adding it to your bill.

🔒

Your data stays yours

Receiver phone numbers are masked from riders. We never sell or share address data.

🤝

One rate card for all

No hidden fees, no secret discounts for the loudest customer, and clear terms in plain language.
