How we weigh

Big, light boxes are charged by size

You pay for the higher of two numbers: the weight on our scale, or the volumetric weight of the box.

A box of cushions might weigh 1.1 kg, but at 40 × 30 × 20 cm it takes the van space of a 4.8 kg parcel, so it is charged as 4.8 kg. Measure the outer box, including any bulges, and divide length × width × height by 5,000.

  • Use the smallest carton that still leaves room for cushioning.
  • Send clothes and soft goods in poly mailers, not boxes.
  • Ship two small boxes instead of one oversized one when it lowers the total.

Billing questions

How charges work after you book

Can't find your answer? Our billing desk replies within one working day at billing@dashmile.in.

Is GST included in the prices on this site?
No. Freight and add-ons are listed before tax and 18% GST is added on the invoice. Registered businesses can claim it as input tax credit. Add your GSTIN in your dashboard settings.
What if your scale disagrees with my weight?
Every parcel is photographed on our calibrated scale at the hub. If the difference changes the price, the photo appears in your dashboard and you have 7 days to dispute it.
How can I pay?
Pay per booking by UPI, card or net banking, or top up a prepaid wallet and get 3% back as credit. Accounts with a GSTIN can apply for 15-day credit terms after three months of regular shipping.
Is there a fuel surcharge or pickup fee?
No. The price shown already covers doorstep pickup within Pune & PCMC and fuel. We review the public rate card once a year, in April, and give 30 days' notice of changes.
What does it cost to cancel after a rider is assigned?
A ₹25 visit fee applies once the rider is on the way. Prepaid amounts, less that fee, are refunded to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.
Are returned (RTO) parcels charged?
Returns are charged at the same freight rate as the outward trip. The COD fee is not charged on parcels that come back undelivered.
