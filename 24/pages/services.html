What we can't carry

These items are refused at pickup to keep riders, sorters and other customers' parcels safe.

  • Cash, coins, bullion and jewellery worth more than ₹50,000
  • Flammables, aerosols, fireworks and gas cylinders
  • Loose lithium batteries (batteries inside devices are fine)
  • Live animals and plants (cut flowers are allowed on local routes)
  • Liquids over 1 litre that aren't in sealed, leak-proof packaging
  • Narcotics, weapons and anything illegal to own in India
