Privacy Policy

A clear, transparent summary of how Glowé collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Information We Collect

When you place an order, book a skin consultation, complete our skin diagnostic, or subscribe to our newsletter, we collect necessary details such as your name, email address, shipping address, phone number, and skincare preferences to provide personalized service and fulfill your orders securely.

Browser Preferences & Storage

We store your preferences (such as your chosen light or dark theme and shopping bag selections) in local browser storage to provide a seamless and responsive browsing experience.

Cookies and Security

We use essential cookies strictly to maintain session state, secure your shopping experience, and protect against fraudulent activity. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Your Rights & Privacy Choices

You have full control over your personal data. You may request access to your information, update your communication preferences, or request deletion of your account details at any time by contacting our support team.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or privacy requests regarding your personal information, please contact us at hello@glowe.com.
