Zero Irritants & Harsh Synthetics

Dermatologically Safe

We strictly exclude compounds known to induce barrier micro-tears, stinging, or trans-epidermal moisture depletion.

  • Harsh Sulfates (SLS, SLES): Strips protective surface sebum and natural acid mantle.
  • Denatured Alcohol: Dissolves essential lipid bilayers, inducing rebound oiliness.
  • Synthetic Colorants & Dyes: Heavy metal residues with zero therapeutic value.
  • Formaldehyde Releasers: Volatile sensitizers banned for cellular safety.

Endocrine & Hormone Safety

Clean Health Commitment

Our safety toxicology panel bars all plasticizers and compounds with suspected bioaccumulation or endocrine interaction.

  • Phthalates (DBP, DEP, DEHP): Endocrine disruptors frequently disguised in perfumes.
  • Parabens (Isobutyl, Butyl): Preservatives linked with hormonal receptor binding.
  • Synthetic Musks: Bioaccumulative synthetic fixatives that resist degradation.
  • Mineral Oil & Petrolatum: Comedogenic heavy refining by-products.

Reef-Safe & Marine Protection

Eco-Conscious Standards

What washes down your drain matters. Every sunscreen and wash formula is certified safe for aquatic life and corals.

  • Oxybenzone & Octinoxate: Bleaches coral reefs and alters marine endocrine balance.
  • Cyclic Silicones (D4, D5): Persistent in marine sediments with extreme aquatic toxicity.
  • Microplastic Beads: Replaced with biodegradable, gentle botanical exfoliants.
  • Animal Testing: Certified 100% Cruelty-Free under the Leaping Bunny standard.
