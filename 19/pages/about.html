Our Story

Started Behind One Counter, Still Run Like One

Founder Elena Ruiz spent a decade restoring stage costumes for Metro City's theatres before opening a 600 sq ft dry-cleaning counter in 2014. Her rules were simple: no harsh solvents, no shortcuts, and every customer greeted by name.

When regulars started asking if we could simply collect their bags from home, we leased two electric vans and ran morning and evening routes ourselves. That doorstep service grew into the AuraWash you know today, but every garment is still cleaned, pressed and checked in our own facilities.

  • Independently owned, never franchised or sold
  • Every order cleaned in-house, never sent to a third-party plant
  • One named care manager for every household account
Milestones

Twelve Years Of Listening To Our Customers

Nearly every step forward started as a request at our counter or a note from one of our drivers. Here is how a single shop became a city-wide doorstep service.

  1. 2014

    The First Counter

    A 600 sq ft dry-cleaning counter opens at 482 Cleanview Boulevard, perchloroethylene-free from its very first garment.

  2. 2016

    Doorstep Vans Hit The Road

    Two leased electric vans start morning and evening pickup routes for our first 40 wash-and-fold subscribers.

  3. 2018

    Westside Tailoring Studio

    Master tailor Clara Novak joins to lead alterations, couture repairs and bridal gown preservation in Bayview Plaza.

  4. 2020

    Contactless By Default

    Porch handoffs, SMS bag photos and 24/7 smart lockers keep wardrobes moving while customers stay home.

  5. 2022

    Rooftop Solar Steam

    Solar thermal panels on the central facility start feeding our pressing and sanitization boilers.

  6. 2024

    Commercial Fleet Dock

    The North Uptown dock opens for hotels, clinics and restaurants that need daily linen and uniform runs.

What We Stand For

Four Promises Every Team Member Signs

They are not wall posters. They decide how we hire, how we train and what happens on the rare day something goes wrong.

Craft Over Shortcuts

Every presser completes 120 hours of in-house training and a supervised 200-garment trial before handling a customer order.

People Who Stay

Our cleaners, spotters and drivers are salaried employees with health cover and paid training. Average tenure: 6.5 years.

Neighbors First

Unclaimed garments are cleaned and donated to Metro City shelters each quarter: more than 3,800 items since 2019.

Own Every Mistake

If something isn't right, a named manager calls you within 24 hours. No ticket numbers, no scripts, no runaround.
