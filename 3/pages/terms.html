Seasonal substitutions

Flowers are natural and availability changes daily. A production store should promise the palette, value, and overall feeling shown, while reserving the right to substitute individual varieties with flowers of equal or greater value.

Same-day orders

Same-day service depends on the published cutoff, delivery capacity, flower availability, and a complete address. Timed delivery should be confirmed separately.

Changes and cancellations

Custom and event work involves advance flower buying. Cancellation windows, non-refundable deposits, and change fees should be stated in the customer’s written proposal.

Problems with an order

Customers should contact the studio within 24 hours with the order number and a clear photo. A fair production policy may offer replacement, store credit, or another appropriate remedy.
