The Commission Experience

From your brief,
to the final stem.

Every custom order is treated as a private artistic commission. Here is how your arrangement progresses from the first message to doorstep arrival.

01

Florist Direction Note

Within 24 hours of receiving your brief, a dedicated florist reviews your requested feeling, checks grower availability, and replies with a palette roadmap and confirmed estimate.

02

Vessel & Mechanics Selection

We select the vessel that elevates your blooms—studio ceramics, artisanal glass, or antiqued metal—engineered exclusively with foam-free brass frogs and fresh water.

03

Dawn Sourcing & Hand-Tying

On the morning of delivery, stems are hand-picked from our grower crates, conditioned at ambient temperature, and arranged stem by stem in natural 360-degree harmony.

04

Studio Preview & Handover

You receive a high-resolution photograph of the completed composition for approval before our in-house courier departs in a climate-guarded vehicle.

Zero Generic Fillers

We never dilute your bespoke budget with cheap dyed carnations or plastic greenery. Every single stem is purposeful.

Handwritten Calligraphy Card

Your personal message is penned by hand on textured 350gsm cotton cardstock and tucked in a sealed wax envelope.

5-Day Studio Freshness Guarantee

If your bespoke arrangement fails to open and thrive beautifully for at least five full days, we replace it with zero hesitation.
