What this demo stores

This website is a static demonstration. Theme preference, layout direction, and the demo cart count may be stored in your browser using local storage. Enquiry and account forms do not send information to a server.

Information a production store may collect

A live flower shop may collect order, contact, recipient, delivery, and payment details required to fulfil purchases. It should collect only what is necessary, explain each purpose, and use a compliant payment provider.

Your choices

  • Clear browser storage at any time through your browser settings.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion from the live store operator.
  • Opt out of marketing messages without affecting order communications.
