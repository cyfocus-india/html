05 / Notes from the flower table

Keep the lovely
going a little longer.

Seasonal flowers keep changing after they arrive. A few small rituals help you enjoy every stage.

  1. A fresh start

    Unwrap gently, trim the stem ends with clean scissors, and place in a clean vase with fresh water. Keep leaves above the waterline.

  2. A little daily attention

    Refresh the water regularly, remove faded blooms, and trim the stems again when you change the water.

  3. A happy place

    Choose a cool spot away from direct sun, heat, and ripening fruit. Give delicate petals a little room to open.
