# Original Image Production Prompts

These five site-level images were produced with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool and saved into `assets/images/` as optimized WebP assets. The six shop-product prompts are recorded separately in `SHOP_IMAGE_PROMPTS.md`.

## `florist-hero.webp`

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: premium flower shop landing page hero
Primary request: an editorial florist studio scene with a fresh, abundant seasonal bouquet being arranged by a florist's hands
Scene/backdrop: intimate sunlit floral studio with a pale plaster wall and a dark walnut worktable
Subject: sculptural bouquet of blush garden roses, burgundy ranunculus, pale peach stock, sweet peas, and airy greenery in a handmade cream ceramic vase
Style/medium: high-end photorealistic editorial lifestyle photography, natural botanical detail
Composition/framing: wide landscape composition, bouquet and hands placed toward the right half with calm usable negative space on the left; subtle foreground petals; elegant depth
Lighting/mood: soft late-morning window light, warm, tactile, quietly luxurious
Color palette: deep plum, blush, peach, butter cream, muted sage
Materials/textures: velvety petals, veined leaves, matte ceramic, warm wood grain, subtle plaster texture
Constraints: no text, no logos, no watermark, no storefront signage, no visible face, realistic hands and stems, suitable for a responsive website hero crop
Avoid: oversaturated colors, plastic-looking petals, generic stock-photo polish, clutter
```
