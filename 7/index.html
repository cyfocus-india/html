What's Inside

GAMES & ATTRACTIONS

Twelve permanent attractions plus a daily rotation of coach-led group games. Everything below is included in a standard entry pass — there are no coin slots or upgrade fees inside.

Mega Ball Pit

50,000 antibacterial balls, a wide tube slide landing and a shallow toddler bay at one end. UV-C washed twice a day.

  • All ages
  • Always open

Slide Tower

Four slides on one tower: a gentle toddler wave, twin racers, a 20-foot spiral drop and a clear acrylic tunnel.

  • Ages 2+
  • Height gated

Climb & Ninja Course

A 14-foot traverse bouldering wall, wobble bridges, monkey bars and a foam landing pit with marshal supervision.

  • Ages 6+
  • Coach on duty

Timed Agility Arena

Electronic light-gate sprint course with a live leaderboard. Kids can race the clock or run head-to-head heats.

  • Ages 8+
  • Leaderboard

Sensory Garden

Textured crawl mats, mirror panels, bubble tubes and a musical touch wall built at floor height for babies.

  • 0 – 2 years
  • Gated area

Role-Play Town

A miniature high street — supermarket, fire station, vet clinic and ice cream van — with costumes and props.

  • 3 – 6 years
  • Costumes

Mini Sports Court

Padded five-a-side pitch that switches between football, basketball hoops and dodgeball for coach-led matches.

  • Ages 5+
  • Team games

Interactive Wall Games

Projection-based target walls, a reaction light grid and an air-hockey pair — no tokens, no extra charge.

SAFETY IS OUR PRIORITY

Every arena is built to ASTM F1918 and BIS 15877 play-equipment standards, and children only leave with the adult whose RFID wristband matches theirs.

  • BIS certified, flame-retardant equipment
  • Shock-absorbent soft play flooring throughout
  • Weekly equipment inspection & daily UV-C ball wash
  • Paired parent–child wristbands at every exit
  • Red Cross certified first-aid coaches on every floor

Good To Know

PLANNING YOUR FIRST VISIT

Four steps from booking to play. Pre-booked families skip the entry queue and go straight through the express turnstile.

  1. 1

    Book your slot

    Pick a date, an age zone and the number of children. Slots run every 90 minutes and live capacity is shown before you pay.

  2. 2

    Sign the digital waiver

    One short online form covers all your registered children for a full year. No paperwork at the desk.

  3. 3

    Scan your QR pass

    Show the QR code from your confirmation at the turnstile. You and your child receive paired RFID wristbands.

  4. 4

    Socks on, play on

    Grip socks are required on the mats for everyone. Bring your own or pick up a pair at reception for $3.50.
