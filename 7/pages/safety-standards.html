Zero Compromise Safety

Safety Is Our #1 Priority

We build and run this arena to international play-park guidance — ASTM F1918 for soft contained play and BIS 15877 for equipment. That means certified materials, a documented inspection routine, trained first-aid staff on every floor, and a child-exit system that cannot be bypassed.

Equipment Checks Daily + weekly audit
Ball Wash UV-C, twice daily
First Aid On every floor
CCTV 100% of play areas
The Standards We Work To

OUR 4 SAFETY PILLARS

Equipment, hygiene, trained staff and controlled entry. Each pillar has a written procedure that our floor team signs off every single day.

Inspected Weekly

BIS certified, flame-retardant equipment

Every slide, ball-pit border, climbing grip and bridge is made from high-density flame-retardant EVA foam and food-grade non-toxic HDPE plastic. There are no sharp metal edges and no exposed bolts anywhere a child can reach.

  • BIS 15877
  • Weekly audit
  • Flame retardant
UV-C Certified Clean

Hospital-grade multi-stage sanitization

Our ball pits go through automated ultraviolet (UV-C) ball washing twice a day. High-touch equipment is steam-sterilised with child-safe organic disinfectant every three hours, and HEPA purifiers run continuously across the arena.

  • Steam sterilised
  • HEPA filtered air
  • Child-safe agents
Red Cross Certified

Certified first-aid & CPR staff

Our floor play coaches hold Red Cross certification in Paediatric First Aid and CPR, renewed annually. We keep an active 1:6 coach-to-child ratio in the baby zones and 1:8 in the senior obstacle zones, with an AED at the first-aid station.

  • AED on site
  • Background checked
  • Annual recert
Anti-Lost Protection

Matching wristbands & 100% CCTV

Parents and children receive cryptographically paired RFID wristbands at entry. No child can leave the turnstile unless the matching adult band is scanned at the same moment, and every square metre of play area is covered by recorded CCTV.

  • Paired bands
  • 24/7 recording
  • Single controlled exit
Our Daily Routine

WHAT HAPPENS EVERY DAY

The checks that run from opening to closing, whether or not anyone is watching.

  1. 1

    08:30 — Open-up inspection

    Duty manager walks all four zones against a 42-point checklist: anchors, netting, padding, stitching and slide surfaces.

  2. 2

    Every 3 hours — Touch-point clean

    Handrails, grips, slide mouths and lockers steam-cleaned with child-safe disinfectant and logged by the cleaning lead.

  3. 3

    13:00 & 20:00 — UV-C ball wash

    Ball pits are drained in rotation and the balls cycled through the ultraviolet washing drum before going back in.

  4. 4

    20:30 — Close-down report

    Any incident, near-miss or worn part is logged. Anything flagged is taken out of service until it is repaired or replaced.

Hygiene & Traction Standard

ANTI-SKID GRIP SOCKS POLICY

For maximum hygiene and fall prevention, grip socks with rubberised bottom treads are strictly required for every child and every adult entering the play arenas. Ordinary socks, shoes and bare feet are not permitted on the mats.

  • Prevents slipping on high-gloss slide dismount ramps
  • Protects the sterile soft-foam vinyl padding from dirt and grit
  • Available at reception in all children's and adult sizes for $3.50
  • Free with every visit on the Platinum Champion membership

Working Together

WHAT WE ASK OF PARENTS

Our team handles the equipment and the hygiene. These four things are yours.

Stay on site

A parent or guardian must remain in the building for the whole session. Children under 4 need an adult inside the zone with them.

Stay home if unwell

Please reschedule free of charge if your child has a temperature, a rash, or has had a stomach upset in the last 48 hours.

Remove loose items

No jewellery, drawstrings, scarves or hard hair clips on the equipment. Free lockers are available at reception.

Tell us what we need to know

Flag allergies, asthma or additional needs at check-in so your child's coach knows before play starts.
