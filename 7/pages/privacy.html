PRIVACY POLICY & CHILD DATA PROTECTION

1. Introduction & Core Philosophy

At PLAY ZONE, the safety and privacy of our children and their families is our highest responsibility. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, handle, and safeguard data acquired through our parent portal, mobile check-in turnstiles, and reservation systems.

2. Information We Collect

When a parent registers an account or signs our digital safety waiver, we collect the following minimum necessary details:

  • Parent / Guardian Name, Email Address, and Primary Emergency Phone Number
  • Registered Children's First Names, Dates of Birth (used strictly for age-appropriate zone access), and any voluntary medical allergy disclosures
  • Session reservation dates, waiver timestamps, and invoice billing records

3. CCTV & RFID Wristband Policy

For the physical security of all minors, PLAY ZONE operates continuous high-definition closed-circuit television (CCTV) throughout all public play arenas.

  • CCTV footage is retained in an encrypted local server for a rolling 30-day period solely for safety auditing, lost item tracking, and incident verification.
  • Footage is strictly confidential and is never marketed, sold, or broadcast publicly.
  • RFID parent-child matching wristbands are erased immediately upon turnstile check-out.

4. Data Protection & Contact

Parents may request the review, correction, or complete deletion of their family profile at any time by contacting our Data Protection Officer at privacy@playzone.com or calling our guest support line at +91 1234 567 890.
