How It Works

MEMBERSHIP IN FOUR STEPS

No paperwork and no joining fee. Your pass is live the moment you sign up.

  1. 1
    Step 01

    Pick your tier

    Choose monthly or annual billing. Annual takes 20% off and is charged once, up front.

  2. 2
    Step 02

    Add your children

    Register each child's name and date of birth so the right age zone opens at the gate.

  3. 3
    Step 03

    Book from the portal

    Member slots open 48 hours before general release, and sessions draw from your monthly balance.

  4. 4
    Step 04

    Scan & play

    Show your digital pass at the turnstile. Gold and Platinum members use the express lane.
