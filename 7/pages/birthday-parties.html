Facilities

WHAT COMES WITH THE ROOM

Everything below is part of the suite booking — no hidden hire charges on the day.

Private lockable suite

Your own room with its own door, seating for up to 40 guests, adjustable lighting and a Bluetooth sound system.

Dedicated Party Captain

One coordinator assigned to your party from arrival to cleanup — runs the games, serves the food and cuts the cake.

In-house certified kitchen

Stone-oven pizza, nuggets, fruit platters and allergen-labelled alternatives, prepared in our own HACCP-certified kitchen.

Themed cake & candles

A two-tier cake matched to your theme, with egg-free and gluten-free options on request at booking.

Photo backdrop & props

A styled backdrop wall with a prop basket, plus a shared digital album of the Party Captain's photos after the event.

Goodie bags & invitations

Unlimited digital invitations with RSVP tracking, plus a themed goodie bag handed to each guest on the way out.

The Run Sheet

HOW A SUPER PLAY PARTY RUNS

A worked example of the most popular two-hour package. Your Party Captain adjusts the timings on the day to suit the age group.

  1. 1

    0:00 — Welcome

    Guests check in at the VIP Party Desk, get wristbands and grip socks, and drop coats and gifts in the suite.

  2. 2

    0:10 — Free play

    Ninety minutes across the age-appropriate zones, with the Party Captain running two group games mid-session.

  3. 3

    1:30 — Food & cake

    Everyone moves into the private suite. Hot food is served, then the lights dim for the cake and candles.

  4. 4

    2:00 — Goodie bags

    Guests collect their goodie bags at the door. We handle every bit of the cleanup once you leave.
