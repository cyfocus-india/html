Our Mission & Purpose

WHERE PLAY COMES TO LIFE

Founded in 2021 by certified child development specialists and a group of fed-up parents, PLAY ZONE grew out of a simple belief: every child deserves a magical, genuinely safe space to burn energy, build confidence and make friends — and every parent deserves somewhere decent to sit while it happens.

Our Origin Story

DESIGNED FOR ACTIVE, SCREEN-FREE CHILDHOODS

In an era dominated by digital screens and sedentary routines, children need real physical spaces to test their balance, sprint without limits and tackle developmental obstacles at their own pace.

So we engineered custom padded towers, tactile climbing elements and sensory infant pools that encourage natural problem solving, agility and social laughter — then wrapped the whole thing in an age-gating system so no toddler ever gets flattened by a nine-year-old on a mission.

Five years on we run seven days a week, host more than a thousand birthdays a year, and still have the same rule we opened with: if a piece of equipment would not be good enough for our own children, it does not go on the floor.

Our 3 Core Commitments

1. Hygiene Without Compromise

Medical-grade UV-C ball washing, continuous HEPA air purifiers, and a steam-clean cycle on every high-touch surface once every three hours.

2. Age-Segregated Safety

Four gated zones with age checks at entry, so toddlers and older kids play in their own space without collision risk.

3. Stress-Free Parents Experience

High-speed Wi-Fi, proper coffee, comfortable working stations and 360-degree sightlines into every zone from the lounge.

Inside The Building

HOW THE FACILITY IS LAID OUT

One floor, one controlled entry point, and everything visible from the parents' lounge.

A building designed around supervision

The arena is deliberately one open floor. There are no blind corners, no separate upstairs rooms and no side exits. From the café lounge a parent can see into every zone, and the only way out of the play area is back through the turnstile you came in by.

  • Four gated play zones covering ages 0 – 12, each with its own equipment and coaches
  • Four private party suites that lock away from the main floor
  • Parents' café lounge with full sightlines, fast Wi-Fi and work desks
  • Nursing room, baby-change and family washrooms beside the Baby Zone
  • Free lockers and stroller parking at reception

Parents' café lounge

Barista coffee, fresh food, high chairs and a quiet work bench with power sockets. Members get 5 – 15% off everything on the menu.

Family facilities

A private nursing room, two baby-change stations, family washrooms and a warm bottle station — all within sight of the Baby Zone.

Lockers & stroller bay

Free full-height lockers for coats, shoes and bags, plus a supervised stroller bay right beside the turnstiles.

Work-friendly Wi-Fi

Full-fibre Wi-Fi across the building. Platinum members get a reserved desk in the quiet corner of the lounge.

Four party suites

Themed, lockable rooms seating up to 40, each with its own sound system, lighting and photo backdrop wall.

Step-free access

Level entry from the car park, step-free routes to every zone, an accessible washroom and a quiet hour every Tuesday morning.

What We Run

MORE THAN OPEN PLAY

Beyond drop-in sessions, our coaches run a weekly programme that is included with every entry.

A programme, not just a playground

Six coach-led sessions run every day and they cost nothing extra — they are part of standard entry. Parents are welcome to join in or watch from the lounge.

  • Toddler sensory rhythm & music — tambourines, ribbon dance and bubble storms for ages 0 – 3
  • Superhero agility race — a timed balance course with medals and sticker rewards
  • Messy art hour — aprons on, paint out, in the Explorer Zone craft corner
  • Glow dance party — neon wristbands, family music and a foam cannon finish
  • School holiday camps — full-day supervised sessions during term breaks
  • Quiet hour — Tuesday mornings with lights and music dimmed, for children who need calmer play

Our People

THE TEAM ON THE FLOOR

Around forty play coaches, party captains, kitchen staff and cleaners keep the building running.

Trained, checked and actually watching

Our coaches are not there to tidy up — they are there to supervise. Every one of them holds a current Red Cross Paediatric First Aid and CPR certificate, renewed annually, and every one has passed a background check before their first shift.

HOW WE GOT HERE
