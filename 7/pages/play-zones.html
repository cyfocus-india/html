Developmentally Engineered Spaces

OUR 4 DEDICATED PLAY ZONES

Every zone is strictly age-segregated behind its own gate, so infants crawl peacefully while older kids conquer high-energy obstacles with zero collisions. Scroll through each zone to see its layout, its equipment and the games your child will actually play.

AGES 0 – 2 YEARS

Baby Zone — Sensory Oasis

A calm, fully-padded sanctuary designed for crawlers, cruisers and early walkers. Everything sits at floor height, every edge is wrapped in soft foam, and the whole zone is behind a self-closing gate that older children cannot open.

Session 90 minutes
Entry $15 per child
Supervision 1 coach : 6 children
  • Low-height foam mirrors and a musical touch wall
  • Self-closing gate keeps older children out
  • Plush toys and play balls UV-sterilised daily
  • Nursing and nappy-change suite directly adjacent
Book Baby Session ($15) Safety Protocols

What your baby plays with

  • Shallow baby ball pool

    Knee-deep pool filled with soft fabric balls, walled so nothing rolls away.

  • Musical touch & mirror wall

    Press-and-play sound panels, safety mirrors and textured shapes at crawling height.

  • Foam crawl tunnel & steps

    Soft arch tunnel and three-step climb with a gentle slope back down.

  • Sensory garden corner

    Bubble tube, textured crawl mats and light panels for quiet, calming play.

AGES 3 – 5 YEARS

Explorer Zone — Imagination Town

A miniature high street where preschoolers build motor coordination and social play. They run the supermarket, drive the fire engine, serve ice cream, then race each other down the double wave slide — all inside one enclosed, coach-supervised area.

Session 90 minutes
Entry $18 per child
Supervision 1 coach : 6 children
  • Double wave slide with deceleration landing pads
  • Giant lightweight interlocking architectural foam bricks
  • Pretend-play fire station, supermarket, vet clinic and ice cream van
  • Dress-up costume rail restocked and laundered daily
Book Explorer Session ($18) View Party Suites

Games in this zone

  • Market & supermarket

    Trolleys, play food, a working till and aprons for shopkeeper role-play.

  • Fire station & vet clinic

    Climb-in fire engine, helmets, soft toy patients and a check-up bench.

  • Foam brick build zone

    Oversized bricks, arches and ramps — light enough for a three-year-old to lift.

  • Double wave mini slide

    Two side-by-side lanes at toddler gradient, with a padded run-off mat.

AGES 6 – 8 YEARS

Adventure Zone — Ninja Cruise

A high-action playground built for energetic primary schoolers. Three levels of ninja crawl, a traverse climbing wall, suspended rope bridges and a foam pit to jump into — with first-aid certified marshals stationed on the floor whenever the zone is open.

Session 2 hours
Entry $20 per child
Supervision 1 marshal : 8 children
  • 14-foot traverse bouldering wall over full crash matting
  • Suspended wobble bridges and monkey bar crossings
  • 20-foot spiral drop slide with transparent acrylic curves
  • Padded five-a-side court for coach-led team games
Book Adventure Session ($20) Equipment Specs

Challenges on the floor

  • Traverse climbing wall

    Colour-graded routes from beginner to hard, all at safe traverse height.

  • Rope bridge & monkey bars

    Wobble planks, cargo netting and a hanging bar crossing between towers.

  • Spiral drop slide

    Two full turns through clear acrylic sections into a deceleration run-out.

  • Foam block jump pit

    Deep pit of soft blocks for supervised dismounts and landing practice.

AGES 9 – 12 YEARS

Challenge Zone — Sky Arena

Built for pre-teens who want a real test. Electronically timed agility runs with a live leaderboard, a vertical spider-web tower, Olympic-grade foam dismount pits and an interactive digital target wall for head-to-head games.

Session 2 hours
Entry $22 per child
Supervision 1 marshal : 8 children
  • Electronic light-gate sprint course with live leaderboard
  • Spider-web vertical climbing tower
  • Olympic-grade foam jump dismount pits
  • Arcade wall, air hockey and interactive digital target games
Book Challenge Session ($22) Get Unlimited Pass

Games & competitions

  • Timed ninja sprint

    Light-gate timing with the day's fastest runs posted on the leaderboard.

  • Spider-web tower

    Vertical rope lattice to the top platform, with a padded base and marshal spot.

  • Interactive target wall

    Projection targets and a reaction light grid for solo scores or team rounds.

  • Arcade & air hockey

    Free-play tables and arcade cabinets — no tokens and no extra charge.

Applies Everywhere

RULES FOR EVERY ZONE

The same four rules apply in all four arenas, whatever your child's age.

Grip socks required

Anti-skid socks on the mats for children and adults alike. Bring your own or buy a pair at reception for $3.50.

Stay in your age zone

Gates are age-checked at entry. It keeps toddlers away from high-speed play and gives older kids room to run.

One adult stays on site

A parent or guardian must remain in the building. Under-4s need an adult inside the zone with them.

Matching wristbands

Children only leave the turnstile when the paired adult wristband is scanned at the same time.
