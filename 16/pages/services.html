What we do

Four crafts, done properly

Every service begins with a conversation about your hair and ends with a finish you can recreate at home.

Haircuts

Precision cuts for every length and texture. We cut wet when a line needs to be exact, and dry when curls, waves and texture need to be seen as they really fall.

Always included

  • Consultation and hair-history check
  • Shampoo with scalp massage
  • A finish you can repeat at home

On the menu

  • Women's cut & style
  • Short cut & style
  • Curly dry cut
  • Men's scissor cut
  • Bang & neckline trims
  • Kids' cuts

Coloring

From covering grays to a full blonde transformation, every formula is built around your skin tone, your starting shade and how often you want to come back.

Always included

  • Patch and strand test for new color clients
  • Your formula saved for next time
  • Toner or gloss, then a blow-dry

On the menu

  • Root touch-up
  • All-over color
  • Partial & full highlights
  • Balayage
  • Gloss & toner
  • Color correction

Beard Trims

Beards are shaped to your jawline and growth pattern, lined up with a straight razor and finished with warm oil — whether you're growing it out or keeping it tight.

Always included

  • Hot towel to soften the hair
  • Straight-razor line-up on cheeks and neck
  • Beard oil and a home-care tip

On the menu

  • Beard trim & line-up
  • Beard sculpt
  • Hot-towel straight-razor shave
  • Head shave
  • Gray blending

Styling

For dinners, weddings or just because: smooth blowouts, bouncy waves, braids and polished updos that hold up long past midnight.

Always included

  • Wash with a treatment matched to your hair
  • Heat protection on every style
  • A day-two refresh tip

On the menu

  • Blowout
  • Waves & curls
  • Updos & event styles
  • Silk press
  • Box braids & cornrows
  • Bridal trials

Add-ons & treatments

Small extras that change how your hair behaves

Add any of these when you book, or ask at the chair — most take fifteen minutes or less.

Bond-repair treatment

Rebuilds the inner structure of bleached or heat-styled hair. Ideal alongside any lightening service.

Deep conditioning mask

Ten minutes under gentle heat for softness and shine that last several washes.

Scalp detox

Lifts product buildup and calms itching, followed by a cooling massage.

Clear gloss

Mirror shine without changing your color; fades gently over about six weeks.

Hot towel & charcoal mask

The barber-side reset: steam, a deep-cleansing mask and a cold towel to finish.

Brow tidy

A quick shape with scissors and razor so your brows match the new cut.

Service finder

Not sure what to book?

Pick what you're hoping for and we'll point you to the right service — plus what to mention in your booking notes.

We suggest

A haircut in your usual style

Book the same cut as last time — Women's, Short, Curly or Men's. We'll refresh the shape, clean up the ends and keep the length you like.

In your notes: roughly when your last cut was, so we can plan the shape around the regrowth.

We suggest

Highlights or balayage

Highlights bring brightness right from the root; balayage is hand-painted and grows out softer, so you can wait longer between visits.

In your notes: any box dye from the last two years — if so, we'll start with a color consultation.

We suggest

Root touch-up or gray blending

A root touch-up gives full coverage every four to six weeks. Gray blending, on the barber side, softens grays so regrowth is barely noticeable.

In your notes: roughly how much gray you have — a quarter, half or most of it.

We suggest

A gloss, or a correction consultation

Brassiness is usually solved with a gloss and toner. Patchy, banded or very dark color needs a correction plan, which starts with a consultation.

In your notes: when your hair was last colored, and whether it was done at home.

We suggest

A beard sculpt

Shape, straight-razor line-up and a hot towel in one appointment. Add a cut if your hair and beard should meet in a seamless fade.

In your notes: how long you've been growing it — two to three weeks of growth gives us more to sculpt.

We suggest

An event style, with a trial first

Book an updo or event style for the day itself, and a trial four to eight weeks beforehand so nothing is left to chance.

In your notes: your neckline, any accessories and what time you need to leave.

We suggest

A free 15-minute consultation

We'll talk through options, upkeep and cost before you commit to anything — then book the right service with the right person.

In your notes: the looks you're drawn to — then bring the photos along to the consultation.

Before & after your visit

Small habits that make the result last

What you do the day before and the week after matters almost as much as the time in the chair. Pick a service for our short checklist.

Haircuts

◷Before you come in

  • Wear your hair the way you usually do, so we can see your part, cowlicks and natural fall.
  • Booked a curly dry cut? Arrive with clean, dry, product-free curls worn down, not tied up.
  • Save two or three reference photos, plus one look you don't want.

↺After you leave

  • Give a new shape two or three washes to settle before you judge it.
  • Start with a pea-sized amount of the product we showed you, and add more only if you need it.
  • Book your next visit at the desk — every 6 to 8 weeks keeps most cuts on track.

Remember the 7-day fix: if something feels off after a few days, come back within a week and we'll adjust it free of charge.

Coloring

◷Before you come in

  • Wash your hair a day or two before. Natural oils help protect your scalp during lightening.
  • New to coloring with us? Pop in for a five-minute patch test at least 48 hours ahead.
  • Tell us about box dye, henna or keratin treatments from the past two years.

↺After you leave

  • Wait 72 hours before your first shampoo so the color can fully set.
  • Switch to a sulfate-free shampoo and rinse with cool water.
  • Use heat protection every time — hot tools fade color faster than anything else.

Keep it fresh: a gloss between color appointments revives the tone and shine in about 45 minutes.

Beard Trims

◷Before you come in

  • Want a new shape? Let it grow for two to three weeks — it gives us more to sculpt.
  • Put the trimmer down for a week, especially around the neckline.
  • Come with it clean and dry, without heavy balm or wax.

↺After you leave

  • Work a few drops of beard oil into the skin underneath every day.
  • Brush it into shape each morning with a boar-bristle brush.
  • Book a line-up every 2 to 3 weeks to keep the edges crisp.

Pair it with a cut: book the cut & beard combo so your fade and beard blend into one seamless line.

Styling

◷Before you come in

  • For updos and event styles, wear a top that buttons or zips so it comes off without touching your hair.
  • Bring any accessories — veil, clips or flowers — so we can build them in.
  • Booked braids? Arrive with your hair detangled. The braiding hair is on us.

↺After you leave

  • Sleep on a silk pillowcase or in a loose bonnet to keep a blowout or silk press smooth.
  • Keep a fresh silk press away from steam and humidity for the first 24 hours.
  • Moisturize your scalp twice a week while in braids, and take them out within 6 to 8 weeks.

Big day coming up? Book a trial 4 to 8 weeks before, so the style is tested and nothing is left to chance.
