Pricing questions

Good to know before you book

Why do salon prices change by stylist level?

Levels reflect experience and demand. Every level uses the same products and standards; Senior and Director stylists simply have more years behind the chair and longer waitlists.

Is there an extra charge for long or thick hair?

For color, hair past the shoulder blades or very dense hair may add $20–$45 for extra product and time. We always confirm it during the consultation, before anything is mixed.

Is a tip included?

No. Tipping is optional and always appreciated, and tips left on a card go 100% to your stylist or barber.

How can I pay?

All major credit and debit cards, contactless phone payments and cash. Gift cards and Fade Club credits are applied at checkout.

Can I get an exact price before booking?

Yes. Email two daylight photos of your hair (front and back) to the front desk and we'll reply with a written price that's honored for 30 days.
