Our story, people and standards

About

Strand & Steel is named for the two halves of the craft: the strand — color, cutting and styling — and the steel — clippers, shears and straight razors. Since 2015 we've kept both under one roof on SE Larkspur Avenue.

How we got here

From two barber chairs to a full house

  1. 2015

    Two chairs and a borrowed sign

    Dorian Vance opens a two-chair barbershop in a former watch-repair shop, starting with his grandfather's hand-me-down chair.

  2. 2017

    Color moves into the back room

    Colorist Hannah Ellis rents the back room, and regulars start booking a fade and a gloss on the same afternoon.

  3. 2019

    Partners, and a wall comes down

    Dorian and Hannah become co-owners, take over the storefront next door and open a single shared front desk.

  4. 2022

    The curl program

    Our dry-cut curl program launches, and every stylist completes in-house certification before cutting textured hair.

  5. 2026

    Sundays, finally

    After a year of Saturday waitlists we add Sunday hours and a second shampoo station.

Our standards

What happens behind the chair matters too

✚

Clean between every client

Shears, combs and clipper blades go through hospital-grade disinfectant after every service; capes and towels are always fresh.

❦

Ingredients you can ask about

Our color and care lines are vegan and cruelty-free, and we'll write down your exact formula whenever you ask.

✎

Always learning

Every team member logs 40+ hours of advanced education a year, then teaches it back to the team on Tuesday mornings.

♻︎

A low-waste color bar

Color is weighed to the gram, and foils, tubes and leftover dye go to a salon recycling program instead of the drain.

Careers

Work on the other side of the cape

We hire for craft and kindness, then invest in both. If you'd rather build a book of loyal regulars than race through a queue, you'll fit right in.

  • Paid education. 40+ hours of advanced classes a year, on the clock and on us.
  • Hourly base plus commission. A guaranteed wage while you build your book, and 15% on every product you sell.
  • Four-day weeks. Choose a four-day schedule — Mondays are closed for everyone.
  • Health stipend and paid time off. A monthly health stipend from day one and two weeks off in your first year.
  • A say in the shop. Product lines, playlists and the education calendar are all decided together.

Senior Colorist

  • Salon side
  • Full-time

Three or more years behind the chair, at home with balayage, blonding and corrections. You'll work alongside Hannah and learn our formula system from day one.

Barber

  • Barber side
  • Full- or part-time

A current Oregon barber license and a steady hand with fades, tapers and the straight razor. Saturdays are a must; Sundays are up to you.

Apprentice Program

  • Both sides
  • 12 months, paid

Just finished school? Train on paid shifts beside Dorian or Hannah, learn our consultation and texture methods, and graduate to a chair of your own.

Interested in one of these roles — or one we haven't listed? Send us your portfolio or Instagram handle and tell us which side of the shop you'd like to work on.
