Getting here

Easy to find, easy to park

By car

Free two-hour street parking on Larkspur and 21st, plus four client spaces in the lot behind the building.

By bike or bus

A bike corral sits right outside, and the Larkspur & 21st bus stop is half a block east.

Step-free access

Level entry from the street, a wide accessible restroom and a height-adjustable chair on each side.

Look for

The copper door between the bookshop and the bakery, with our scissors sign hanging above it.

Booking policies

A few fair rules that keep the day running

Cancellations

Please give 24 hours' notice. Later cancellations are charged 50% of the service; missed color appointments are charged in full.

Running late

We'll always try to fit you in. After 15 minutes we may need to shorten the service or move you to the next free slot.

Deposits

Color services over three hours and all event bookings need a $50 deposit, credited in full to your final bill.

Patch tests

New color clients need a quick patch test at least 48 hours before the appointment — drop in any time we're open.

Under 16s

Clients under 16 need a parent or guardian with them for the whole appointment.
