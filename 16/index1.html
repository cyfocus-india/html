Weddings & events

Hair for the big day — and everyone in the photos

From one bride to a full wedding party, we style here at the studio or on location, while the barber side takes care of the groom's crew.

  1. Trial · 6–8 weeks before

    A full rehearsal of your style, photographed from every angle so we can fine-tune it together.

  2. Plan · 2 weeks before

    We map a timetable for every person so the last style is finished 30 minutes before you leave.

  3. The day

    An early studio opening or on-site styling within 25 miles, plus touch-up kits for the evening.

Grooms and groomsmen: cuts and straight-razor shaves the day before or on the morning itself.

Walk-in guide

When to drop in on the barber side

Walk-ins are welcome Tuesday to Saturday until 6 pm. Here's the usual wait for a chair, taken from last season's sign-in board.

  • Quietest: Tuesday and Wednesday mornings — you'll usually sit straight down.
  • Busiest: Friday after work and Saturday before 3 pm. On a schedule? Book ahead.
  • While you wait: add your name to the board by the door and help yourself to an espresso.

Typical wait for a barber chair

  • Quiet · under 10 min
  • Steady · 10–25 min
  • Busy · 30+ min
