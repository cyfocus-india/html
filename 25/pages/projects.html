Featured Case Study
Highland Park • 2025

Rewiring a 1930s Craftsman Without Tearing Out Its Plaster

The Challenge

The Vance family's 2,200 sq ft craftsman still ran on original knob-and-tube wiring. Their insurer gave them 90 days to replace it, and they wanted the original plaster and trim kept intact.

Our Solution

We mapped every circuit, fished new copper cable through existing cavities from the attic and basement, and added a 200A panel with AFCI and GFCI protection. Only 11 small access holes were needed.

The Result

Finished in six working days, passed city inspection on the first visit, and the family's homeowner policy renewed at a lower premium.
