Immediate Safety

Emergency Electrical Safety Protocol

If you are experiencing active electrical hazards while awaiting technician arrival, follow these critical safety measures immediately.

⚡

Shut Off Main Breaker

If safe and the area around the panel is completely dry, switch off the large two-pole main breaker to kill power to the whole building.

🚫

Avoid Standing Water

Never step into a flooded basement or garage with submerged electrical cords. Water conducts current and can cause fatal electric shock.

🔌

Unplug Smoking Fixtures

If an appliance is hot, smoking, or sparking, disconnect it immediately if you can safely reach the plug without touching bare wire.

🧯

Use Class-C Fire Extinguishers

Never throw water on an electrical fire. Only use dry chemical Class-C fire extinguishers or evacuate immediately and call 911.
