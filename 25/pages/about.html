Our Story

Built on the Jobs Other Contractors Rushed

Founder Daniel Reyes spent his early years in the trade fixing other people's shortcuts: overloaded panels, unpermitted additions, and aluminum splices hidden behind drywall. In 2004 he opened VoltGuard with a simple idea. Do it right the first time, explain every price before work starts, and leave the house cleaner than you found it.

Two decades later, that idea has grown into 18 licensed electricians, a 14-truck fleet stocked with 500+ OEM parts, and a 24/7 dispatch desk staffed by real people. Every lead technician still holds a state master license, and every job is still backed by our written 5-year workmanship guarantee.

"We'd rather lose a job than cut a corner. Your family sleeps next to the wiring we install." Daniel Reyes, Founder & Master Electrician

Before Your Electrician Arrives

A few minutes of prep helps us diagnose faster and keeps your visit on budget.

  • ✓ Clear a 3-foot path to your electrical panel
  • ✓ Note which breakers trip, and when it happens
  • ✓ Snap a photo of the panel label or fixture box
  • ✓ Keep pets in a separate room during the visit
  • ✓ Have appliance or charger model numbers handy
  • ✓ Share gate codes or parking notes when booking
