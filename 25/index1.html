A GFCI (Ground Fault Circuit Interrupter) protects people from electrical shocks by detecting current leakage (such as when water meets electricity in kitchens and bathrooms). An AFCI (Arc Fault Circuit Interrupter) detects unintended electrical sparks and micro-arcs inside walls caused by damaged cords or loose screw terminals, preventing electrical structure fires.
