Safety Audit Checklist

Warning Signs You Shouldn't Ignore

Early detection prevents residential electrical fires. If you notice any of these symptoms, disconnect the appliance and call our experts immediately.

⚠️

Buzzing or Crackling at the Breaker Box

Indicates dangerous electrical arcing across a degraded breaker contact or loose main neutral wire that can ignite panel insulation.

Action: Disconnect main power if safe and call for emergency dispatch.
💡

Flickering or Dimming When Appliances Start

Occurs when heavy equipment (air conditioners, microwaves) creates severe voltage drops on shared, under-sized circuits.

Action: Requires a dedicated 20A or 240V branch line to balance electrical load.
🔥

Warm or Discolored Receptacle Plates

Signifies excessive thermal resistance and resistive heating behind the drywall, commonly caused by corroded terminal screws.

Action: Discontinue outlet use immediately and request junction box inspection.
🔌

Outdated Two-Prong Un-Grounded Outlets

Older homes without equipment grounding expose modern electronics, computers, and residents to shock risks during faults.

Action: Upgrade to modern GFCI / AFCI receptacles for whole-room code protection.
