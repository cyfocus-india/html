How the night runs

An evening at Marigold Grand, hour by hour

This is how a typical dinner-slot reception unfolds, and who handles each part so you don't have to.

  1. 3:30 pm

    The décor crew takes over the hall

    Every dinner booking includes a 3½-hour setup window. Florists, lighting and the LED wall go up while you're still at the salon.

  2. 6:00 pm

    Final walkthrough with your event manager

    Sound check, stage seating, the cake table and the family seating plan are signed off together. Changes happen now, not mid-event.

  3. 7:00 pm

    Guests arrive to valets and welcome drinks

    Valets take cars at the porch, and chilled mocktails are served in the foyer while a live saxophonist plays.

  4. 8:00 pm

    Your stage moments

    The couple's entry, speeches and a photo slideshow on the LED wall. Our floor captain cues each moment with your emcee.

  5. 8:45 pm

    Buffet and live counters open

    Separate veg and non-veg lines, plus a seated counter for elders and children, keep queues under five minutes even at 600 guests.

  6. 11:30 pm

    Last song and a careful wrap-up

    Return gifts are handed out at the exit, and untouched food is packed for a city food bank, with your consent.
