What every booking already includes

Many venues quote low and add extras later. Our hall price covers the essentials below, whichever hall or package you choose.

  • Stage with basic backdrop and lighting
  • Round tables, chiavari chairs and linen
  • Two air-conditioned green rooms
  • Sound system with two cordless mics
  • Valet parking for three hours
  • An event manager and floor captain
  • Menu tasting for six people
  • A welcome drink for every guest
  • Housekeeping and washroom attendants
  • Generator backup, AC and CCTV security
  • Dressed cake and gift tables
  • Drinking-water stations in every hall

The only line we add to your quote is 18% GST. No service charge, no cleaning fee, no surprise "décor handling" costs.

Questions, answered

What hosts usually ask before booking

Straight answers on outside vendors, alcohol, timings and refunds.

No. All food is cooked in our kitchens so we can guarantee hygiene and timing. You're welcome to bring a wedding cake, sweets from your family's favourite shop, or a paan counter.

Yes. Outside vendors pay a one-time access fee of ₹15,000 and follow our setup rules: no nails or tape on walls, and open flames only in approved holders. The fee is waived for our listed partners.

Yes, at dinner events in the Ballroom, Saffron Hall and Ember Room. You buy from a licensed retailer, we help you apply for the one-day excise licence, and our bar team serves for a flat ₹12,000 setup fee.

Indoor halls can play until 11:30 pm. Music on Jasmine Lawn ends at 10 pm, in line with city rules for open-air venues, after which guests can move indoors if a hall is free.

From June to October we recommend our waterproof hanger. At any other time of year, if rain is forecast we move you into Saffron Hall at no extra cost, as long as it's free that day.

Yes, up to 7 days before the event. We bill on the higher of your final confirmed count or the hall's minimum plate guarantee.

Cancel 90 or more days ahead for a full refund minus ₹10,000. Between 30 and 90 days, we refund 50% of what you've paid. Inside 30 days there's no refund, but you can move to another date within six months, once.
