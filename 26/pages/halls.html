Seating styles

Choose the layout that suits your crowd

The same hall feels very different depending on how it's set. Here's what we recommend, and when.

Round banquet

Tables of 8 to 10 around a dance floor. Best when guests eat seated, as at receptions and galas.

Best inAll halls except Ember

Theatre

Rows of chairs facing the stage, for ceremonies, award nights and keynotes with a clear sightline.

Best inBallroom · Saffron · Lotus

Cocktail

High tables and lounge corners with roaming servers. Fits about 50% more guests and keeps everyone mingling.

Best inAll five halls

Classroom

Tables facing forward with a power point on every row. Good for training days and AGMs.

Best inBallroom · Saffron · Lotus

U-shape

Tables in a U around a screen, for board meetings and workshops where everyone needs to speak.

Best inEmber · Lotus
