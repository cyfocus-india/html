About

Family-run, and hosting Bengaluru since 2011

The Rao family built Marigold Grand to give every host the unhurried, well-looked-after celebration they wished they'd had. Meet the people, the promises and the numbers behind it.

Founded 2011 Owned by the Rao family Kaikondrahalli, Sarjapur Road
Our story

It began with one ballroom on the family's mango orchard

Ramesh and Lalitha Rao opened the Marigold Ballroom in 2011 on land their family had farmed for two generations. They had planned too many family weddings in hotel ballrooms that rushed guests out at midnight, and wanted a venue where a family could take its time.

The Saffron Hall followed in 2014, the Jasmine Lawn in 2016, and the Lotus and Ember Rooms in 2019 for smaller gatherings. Their son Karthik now runs our production team, and Ramesh still greets guests at the door most Saturdays.

Our golden rule: one event per hall, per slot. Your family and decorators have the hall from the first minute of your slot, with no other party packing up around you.

What we stand for

Four promises we have kept since day one

The Raos wrote these down before the first booking. Every new team member still learns them in week one.

⚖︎

Honest advice first

If your 380 guests fit the Saffron Hall, we won't push you into the Ballroom. We suggest the hall that suits your list, even when it means a smaller booking.

✿

Your customs, your clock

A dawn muhurtham sets our schedule, not the other way round. We keep ritual checklists for Kannadiga, Tamil, Telugu, Marwari, Bengali and Christian weddings.

♻︎

Nothing goes to waste

Untouched surplus food reaches a partner food bank within two hours of service. Wet waste is composted on campus, and water is served in steel, not plastic.

✦

People who stay

Most of our team has been with us for five years or more, and every new server shadows ten events before working a floor on their own.

Our team

The people who will look after your day

Four heads of department, and at least one of them is on the floor at every event we host.

Suresh Kumar

Director of Banquet Operations

Suresh writes the minute-by-minute run sheet for every booking, walks it through with your family a week before, and stays on the floor from setup to send-off.

Chef Raghavendra Bhat

Executive Chef

Trained in Udupi temple kitchens before moving to five-star banqueting. He writes every menu card himself and tastes each live counter before the doors open.

Priya Raghavan

Head of Décor & Stage Design

Leads our décor studio and in-house florists. Priya turns your mood board into a measured stage drawing, so you see exactly what you'll get before setup day.

Karthik Rao

AV & Production Lead

The founders' son grew up backstage here. Karthik rehearses every entry, speech and slideshow with your emcee or DJ the evening before your event.
