Your information

Privacy policy.

Clear commitments on how Oven & Bloom collects, protects, and respects your personal and celebration order details.

Information we collect

When you send an enquiry or place an order, we may receive your name, email address, phone number, delivery information, event date, design references, dietary notes, and payment status. We also may collect basic device and website-use information through privacy-conscious analytics.

How we use information

  • To answer enquiries and prepare cake proposals.
  • To make, deliver, and support confirmed orders.
  • To send service updates and requested marketing messages.
  • To maintain security, prevent misuse, and understand website performance.

Forms and third-party services

The demo forms in this template do not transmit information until a site owner connects a verified endpoint. A production site may use Formspree, Netlify Forms, Mailchimp, ConvertKit, Stripe, PayPal, maps, or analytics providers. Each service should be listed here with a link to its own privacy terms.

Cookies and local storage

This template stores theme and text-direction preferences in your browser. These values do not identify you and are not sent to the bakery. Add a consent mechanism before enabling non-essential analytics or advertising cookies where required.

Retention and security

Order records should be retained only as long as needed for service, tax, legal, and dispute-resolution purposes. Access should be limited to people who need it, and connected systems should use strong passwords and multifactor authentication.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal from marketing by contacting privacy@ovenandbloom.example. Applicable rights depend on your location.

Children

This website is intended for adults ordering bakery products. It should not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Changes

Update the effective date and this notice whenever collection practices or connected providers materially change.
