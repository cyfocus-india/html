Delivery questions.

Can you guarantee an exact delivery time?

Standard slots are windows because traffic varies. For weddings and venue handoffs, we build a more precise arrival plan and include the setup timing in your quote.

What if nobody is available to receive the cake?

We call the order contact at arrival. Redelivery may carry an additional charge, and cakes cannot be left unattended for food-safety reasons.

Do you deliver outside Bengaluru?

Most finished cakes are too delicate for long-distance transport. Contact us with the venue; simple travel cakes may be possible by special arrangement.

Tell us where the party is.

Share the date, venue, and cake size. We will confirm the safest delivery plan and exact fee.
