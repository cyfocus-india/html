Terms & conditions.

Transparent policies on custom cake orders, celebration tastings, deposits, delivery, and studio care.

Using this website

Website content is provided for general information. Product images are illustrative; handmade goods naturally vary in colour, placement, and finish. Prices and availability may change until an order is confirmed.

Quotes and bookings

Custom quotations are valid for the period stated on the quote. A booking is confirmed only after the required deposit is received and acknowledged. Dates remain available to other customers until confirmation.

Payments

Signature products are normally paid in full at order. Custom orders require a 50% deposit, with the balance due 48 hours before fulfilment unless stated otherwise. Connect only a verified payment provider before production launch.

Changes and cancellations

Reasonable changes may be accepted before production begins and can affect price. Deposits may become non-refundable once ingredients are ordered, design work begins, or the reserved production date cannot be resold. State exact cancellation windows in the final business policy.

Allergens and dietary requests

The bakery handles wheat, eggs, dairy, nuts, soy, and other allergens. “Eggless,” “gluten-aware,” or similar descriptions do not guarantee an allergen-free environment. Customers are responsible for sharing material allergies before ordering.

Collection and delivery

Customers should arrive within their confirmed collection window and transport cakes on a flat, cool surface. Delivery windows account for traffic and weather. The bakery is not responsible for damage after a successful handoff or for deterioration caused by improper storage.

Design and creative discretion

Reference images are treated as inspiration rather than exact copies. The bakery may adapt colour, proportion, materials, and placement to suit food safety, structure, availability, and its own creative style.

Website materials

Unless otherwise stated, original branding, text, and design materials belong to the site owner. Third-party image, font, icon, and library rights remain with their respective owners.

Liability

To the extent permitted by applicable law, liability related to an order should not exceed the amount paid for that order. Nothing here limits rights that cannot legally be excluded.

Contact

Questions may be sent to hello@ovenandbloom.example or Oven & Bloom, 18 Lavender Lane, Indiranagar, Bengaluru 560038.
