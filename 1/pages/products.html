Good to know

A few sweet details.

How much notice do ready-to-order cakes need?

Most signature cakes need 24 to 48 hours. A small same-day selection is available until sold out; call us before noon for the best choice.

Can you make cakes without eggs or gluten?

Many flavours are available eggless. Gluten-aware options are made with care, but our kitchen handles wheat and cannot guarantee zero cross-contact.

How should I store my cake?

Keep it chilled, then bring it to room temperature 30 to 60 minutes before serving for the best texture and flavour.
