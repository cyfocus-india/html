Pricing questions

What shapes the quote?

Why do custom cakes cost more?

Your quote covers design time, small-batch baking, structural planning, hand decoration, and any one-off details created specifically for your cake.

Can you work to a fixed budget?

Yes. Share a comfortable range and we will recommend the size, flavour, and finish that create the strongest result within it.

What happens if I need to change the date?

We will always try to move your booking when production capacity allows. Deposits become non-refundable once ingredient ordering and design work begin.

Ready for an exact quote?

Tell us the date, servings, flavour, and feeling. We will bring the numbers—and the cake—into focus.
