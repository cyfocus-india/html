About Us

We build every gathering around a cake.

Custom celebration cakes are still the heart of Oven & Bloom — hand-piped, made to order, and finished the morning they're collected. Our breads and pastries simply keep the counter sweet in between.

The first batch

One tiny oven. Many crowded dinners.

Oven & Bloom began with founder Naina Rao baking birthday cakes after work and carrying them across Bengaluru in the back seat of a yellow hatchback.

Friends asked for friends. Sketches arrived on napkins. What started as a weekend rhythm grew into a sunny Indiranagar kitchen where celebration cakes share the oven with croissants, tea loaves, and tomorrow's breakfast.

We are still guided by the same question Naina asked at those first dinners: what would make this table feel more loved?

What stays constant

Good cake has principles.

Not rules for the sake of rules. Commitments that you can taste in the crumb and feel in the service.

Flavour before fashion

Every decoration earns its place by supporting the cake beneath it. Real butter, real fruit, balanced sweetness.

Made by people

We mix, fold, pipe, paint, and pack by hand. Small variation is the signature of something truly handmade.

Close to home

We buy locally when the season allows, know our regulars by name, and keep delivery intentionally local.

Inside every slice

Fewer shortcuts.
Better crumbs.

We choose cultured butter, pure vanilla, fine chocolate, ripe local fruit, and spices ground while they still smell alive. Then we give each batch the time it asks for.

A useful kind of sweetness

Baking beyond the box.

Our kitchen works to waste less and share more.

  • Unsold bakes are donated through a local food-sharing partner.
  • Boxes use recyclable paper and plant-based window film where available.
  • Seasonal fruit comes from nearby growers before imported alternatives.
  • Monthly kitchen sessions support young hospitality trainees.
