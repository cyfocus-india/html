The bespoke process

From private tasting box to your wedding table.

Commissioning a bespoke wedding or milestone cake should be as delightful as the first slice. Here is how we collaborate from inquiry to celebration day.

1

Curated Tasting Box

Choose 4 signature flavor combinations. We ship a beautifully packaged cold tasting box straight to your home.

2

Design Consultation

Meet in person at our Indiranagar studio or via video call to discuss your color palette, florals, tier scale, and venue ambiance.

3

Sketch & Proposal

Receive a full digital illustration and transparent quote covering tiers, custom sugar or fresh florals, and transport logistics.

4

On-Site Venue Setup

On your wedding day, our pastry chefs hand-deliver in refrigerated transit and assemble the tiers at the venue with fresh florals.

Tier planning guide

Selecting the right tier scale for your venue.

Every wedding cake tier proportion is calibrated to command the ballroom while providing the exact number of generous dessert portions.

Intimate

1-Tier Petite

Serves 15–20 Guests

Ideal for registry weddings, elopements, intimate family celebrations, or multi-dessert tables.

8" diameter · 5" height · 1 flavor
Boutique

2-Tier Classic

Serves 35–50 Guests

Our most requested scale for garden ceremonies, cocktail receptions, and milestone anniversaries.

6" + 8" tiers · 10" height · Up to 2 flavors
Ballroom

3-Tier Grand

Serves 75–100 Guests

A commanding centerpiece tailored for hotel ballrooms, palace venues, and grand evening receptions.

6" + 8" + 10" tiers · 16" height · Up to 3 flavors
Showstopper

4-Tier Couture

Serves 120–180+ Guests

Dramatic scale with wafer paper sculptures, cascading fresh botanicals, and bespoke color washes.

6" + 8" + 10" + 12" tiers · 22" height · Custom flavors
