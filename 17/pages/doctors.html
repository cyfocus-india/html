Team Approach

How Our Specialists Work Together on Your Care

When a treatment involves more than one specialty, you get one coordinated plan instead of separate opinions.

Weekly Case Reviews

Complex cases, such as implants after orthodontics, are reviewed by all four doctors every Tuesday before your plan is finalized.

One Shared Patient Record

Your X-rays, scans and notes live in one chart, so you never repeat your history or pay for duplicate imaging.

The Same Doctor Throughout

The specialist who plans your treatment also performs it and sees you at every follow-up, so nothing gets lost in a handover.

Patient Experience

Our Unhurried Consultation Standards

Great clinical outcomes begin with thoughtful listening. We structure our daily schedule so your doctor is never rushed between operatory chairs.

Standard 1

45-Minute Dedicated Appointments

Initial consultations are blocked for a full 45 minutes of direct doctor contact. You have ample time to review digital imaging, ask detailed questions, and discuss anxieties without being hurried.

Standard 2

High-Definition Chairside Visualization

We use miniature intraoral HD cameras to show you magnified live video of your teeth on a chairside screen. You see exactly what the doctor sees, turning your diagnosis into a collaborative discussion.

Standard 3

Multi-Pathway Written Options

We never pressure you into a single high-cost procedure. You receive a written comparison of conservative, phased, and comprehensive treatment options with clear prognoses for each choice.
