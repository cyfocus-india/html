Who We Are

From a Two-Chair Practice to a Full Specialist Clinic

Dr. Marcus Vance opened Lumina on Medical Plaza Way in 2011 with a simple idea: nervous patients deserve more time, not less. The first clinic had two treatment chairs, one hygienist and a front desk that doubled as the waiting room.

Word spread through families, neighbors and local schools. Over the years we welcomed an orthodontist, a cosmetic dentist and a pediatric dentist, so braces, implants, veneers and children's care now happen under one roof, with one shared record and no outside referrals.

What has not changed is how we work: longer appointments, plain-language explanations and a written estimate before any treatment begins.

Our Journey

How Lumina Has Grown Since 2011

Every new specialist, room and piece of equipment was added because our patients asked for it.

  1. 2011

    Lumina Opens Its Doors

    Dr. Marcus Vance opens a two-chair practice on Medical Plaza Way, offering check-ups, fillings and oral surgery.

  2. 2014

    Orthodontics Joins the Team

    Dr. Sarah Lin launches our clear aligner and ceramic braces program for teens and adults.

  3. 2016

    A Dedicated Cosmetic Suite

    Dr. Elena Rostova brings porcelain veneers, tooth-colored fillings and in-office whitening in-house.

  4. 2018

    Children's Care and Same-Day Crowns

    Dr. Julian Chen opens our child-friendly treatment room, and chairside milling lets us fit permanent crowns in a single visit.

  5. 2022

    3D Imaging and Guided Implants

    Cone-beam 3D scans and custom surgical guides make implant placement faster, gentler and more precise.

  6. 2026

    18,000 Smiles and Counting

    Four specialists, eight treatment rooms and an online patient portal for bookings, prescriptions and receipts.
