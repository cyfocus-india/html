Financial Planning

Three Smart Ways to Lower Your Out-of-Pocket Costs

Our dedicated treatment coordinators work directly with you and your insurance provider to maximize benefits and avoid out-of-pocket waste.

Strategy 1

Year-End Benefit Splitting

For multi-stage treatments like implants or cosmetic crowns, we can strategically schedule appointments across December and January to leverage two separate annual insurance maximums, potentially doubling your insurance coverage.

Strategy 2

Prior-Authorization Pre-Determination

Before undergoing any major restorative work, we submit your digital X-rays and detailed clinical narrative to your carrier. You receive a formal, binding pre-approval letter confirming exact dollar allowances in advance.

Strategy 3

Pre-Tax HSA & FSA Optimization

100% of our clinical procedures qualify for Health Savings Accounts and Flexible Spending Accounts. Paying with pre-tax dollars effectively discounts your dental treatment by 20% to 35% based on your tax bracket.
