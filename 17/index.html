How It Works

Your First Visit in Four Simple Steps

New patients always know what happens next, what it costs and how long it takes before any treatment begins.

  1. 01

    Gentle Check-Up

    A 60-minute exam with low-dose digital X-rays, gum measurements and an oral cancer screening.

  2. 02

    Clear Smile Plan

    We show you your own photos and X-rays, then walk through every option with written costs and timelines.

  3. 03

    Comfortable Treatment

    Care at your pace with numbing gel before every injection and a hand signal to pause at any time.

  4. 04

    Lasting Results

    A personal home-care routine and six-month recall reminders sent straight to your patient dashboard.

Anxiety-Free Visits

Noise-Canceling Audio

Wireless headphones with your own playlist or calming soundscapes mask the sound of instruments.

💺

Heated Massage Chairs

📺

Ceiling-Mounted Screens

Clinical Excellence

The Four Guarantees Behind Every Visit

We hold our practice to hospital-grade hygiene, biocompatible materials and complete fee transparency.

Safety

Class-B Sterilization

Biological autoclave spore testing is logged every morning. Every instrument cassette remains sealed until opened right in front of you.

Materials

100% Mercury-Free

We use exclusively biocompatible, tooth-matching composite resins and German ceramic restorations that bond naturally with tooth enamel.

Transparency

No Surprise Billing

You receive an itemized, written cost breakdown with insurance benefits verified before any doctor picks up an instrument.

Availability

Same-Day Emergencies

Two emergency slots are kept open daily so patients with acute pain, chipped teeth, or lost crowns receive prompt relief.

New Patients Welcome
