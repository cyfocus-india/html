Pure Ingredients

Rooted in Organic Agriculture

We believe extraordinary desserts start with ethical farming. That is why we partner directly with certified organic family farms within 100 miles of our parlours.

✔
100% Pasture-Raised Organic Milk

Never treated with artificial growth hormones or preventative antibiotics. Cows graze freely on pesticide-free clover pastures.

✔
Direct-Trade Cacao & Bourbon Vanilla

We pay 40% above fair-trade premiums directly to cooperative farmers in Madagascar, Ecuador, and Tahiti.

✔
Zero Synthetic Additives

No high-fructose corn syrup, artificial food colorings, carrageenan, or polysorbates. Every color comes straight from nature.

✔
Compostable Packaging Guarantee

Every cup, spoon, napkin, and pint tub is made from plant starches and certified 100% commercially compostable.
