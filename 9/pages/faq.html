Clinical Care Level Framework

Helping Families Choose the Right Level of Support

Not sure what level of in-home care your loved one requires? Use our clinical acuity framework to identify the right clinician qualifications, support intensity, and scheduling cadence.

Tier 01 High Clinical Acuity
Supervising BSN, RN / CWON

Acute Post-Surgical & Hospital Transition

For patients recently discharged following orthopedic surgery, cardiac events, or complex hospitalizations needing sterile wound care and medical stabilization.

Complexity: Surgical drains, IV infusion, vitals telemetry
Cadence: Daily or multi-hour skilled nurse shifts
Duration: 2 to 6 weeks transitional window
  • 72h Post-discharge surgeon protocol syncing
  • Aseptic dressing changes & suture line tracking
  • Medication reconciliation to eliminate readmission
Tier 02 Moderate Acuity
Certified Nursing Aide (CNA)

Daily Living, Personal Care & Dignity

For seniors wishing to age safely in place with dependable physical support for bathing, transferring, meal preparation, and heartfelt companionship.

Complexity: ADL assistance, transfer guidance, nutrition
Cadence: Flexible 3–8 hour daytime or evening blocks
Duration: Ongoing long-term household presence
  • Safe shower & dignified hygiene assistance
  • Fall prevention & gentle mobility walking support
  • Prescribed meal timing & hydration monitoring
Tier 03 Specialized Cognitive
Certified Dementia Practitioner (CDP)

Cognitive Preservation & Memory Care

For individuals navigating Alzheimer’s, vascular dementia, or Lewy body disorders who benefit from calm routines, validation, and de-escalation.

Complexity: Sensory engagement, sundowning intervention
Cadence: Daily predictable shifts or family respite
Duration: Continuous adaptive progression support
  • Validation therapy & gentle sensory activities
  • Circadian rhythm routines & wandering deterrents
  • Compassionate respite for family care partners
Tier 04 24/7 Comprehensive
Dual-Shift Care Pod & RN Oversight

24/7 Continuous Residential Oversight

For individuals requiring round-the-clock safety, overnight repositioning, severe mobility deficits, or comprehensive end-of-life palliative comfort.

Complexity: Total ADL care, aspiration precautions, telemetry
Cadence: 24-hour continuous dual-clinician rotation
Duration: Uninterrupted day and overnight coverage
  • Overnight 2-hour repositioning & skin protection
  • Continuous vital telemetry with instant RN escalation
  • 100% Guaranteed shift coverage with zero gaps
