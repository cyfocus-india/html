How We Initiate In-Home Care

From the first hospital discharge call to your ongoing daily shift notes, every step is coordinated with clinical accuracy.

1

Initial Clinical Intake

Our RN Intake Coordinator reviews doctor orders, discharge summaries, and home safety requirements.

2

In-Home Assessment

A Senior Nurse meets family at the bedside to assess mobility, medications, vitals baseline, and lifestyle goals.

3

4

Continuous Oversight

Daily electronic shift notes, real-time family updates, and monthly supervisory audits ensure consistent quality.
