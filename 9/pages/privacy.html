Privacy Policy

CareWell respects your privacy. This policy explains the information this website may collect, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

When you submit a care inquiry, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, service needs, and a message. Please do not include detailed medical records in the website form.

How information is used

  • To respond to care questions and consultation requests.
  • To coordinate requested services and communicate with you.
  • To maintain website security, performance, and accessibility.
  • To comply with applicable legal and safety obligations.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers or care team members who need it to respond, operate the service, or meet a legal obligation. Records are retained only as long as reasonably necessary.

Cookies and preferences

This website may store your selected theme and text direction in your browser. These preferences improve your experience and do not identify you personally.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete information you submitted through this website, subject to legal and care-record requirements.

Contact us

For a privacy question, email info@carewellhome.com or write to 123 Carewell Lane, San Francisco, CA 94107.
