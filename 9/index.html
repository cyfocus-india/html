Clinical Transition Care Pathway

Hospital-to-Home Recovery Continuum

A physician-coordinated clinical model ensuring safe discharge, intensive post-acute stabilization, and progressive rehabilitation in the comfort of home.

All Clinical Services
01 Hospital
48 Hours Pre-Discharge

Bedside Consultation & Care Planning

Our Clinical Nurse Supervisor coordinates directly with your hospital discharge planner, surgical team, and attending physician to audit orders and build an individualized home recovery protocol.

  • Surgical order & medication chart reconciliation
  • DME (Durable Medical Equipment) pre-order checklist
  • Bedside patient & family transition briefing
Pre-Discharge Alignment
02 Transfer Day
Hour 0 – Patient Arrival

In-Home Medical & Safety Staging

Before the patient enters the home, our clinical team sanitizes the care suite, stages hospital-grade bedding, verifies fall-prevention pathways, and connects telemetry vital monitoring equipment.

  • Sterile dressing & sharps waste station setup
  • Zero-fall environmental audit & pathway clearance
  • 24/7 Clinical Coordinator emergency line activation
Home Environment Certified
03 Acute Phase
First 72 Hours Post-Op

72-Hour Intensive RN Stabilization

The first 72 hours carry the highest readmission risk. Dedicated Registered Nurses manage wound drains, titration schedules, surgical site asepsis, and round-the-clock vital telemetry checks.

  • Continuous vitals telemetry (BP, SpO2, Temp, Glucose)
  • Drain output quantification & sterile wound care
  • Direct surgeon daily report & symptom alerts
Active Clinical Telemetry
04 Restorative
Weeks 2 to 6+

Targeted Rehabilitation & Autonomy

Transitioning from acute bedside care into active independence. Licensed physical and occupational therapists guide progressive mobility exercises, safe transfers, and daily self-care empowerment.

  • Surgeon-directed gait, balance & strength retraining
  • ADL (Activities of Daily Living) independence mastery
  • Family caregiver coaching & graduated step-down plan
