Anatomy & Physiology

The Science of Intentional Down-Regulation

Modern lives bombard the sensory system. Our evidence-informed sequencing works with your biological mechanisms to restore systemic homeostasis.

♦

Vagus Nerve Tone

Extended exhalations during ujjayi breathing stimulate the vagus nerve, lowering arterial blood pressure and decreasing resting heart rate within minutes.

♧

Fascial Hydration

Sustained 3- to 5-minute yin holds mechanically stress collagen fibers, stimulating hyaluronic acid production to rehydrate stiff joint capsules.

♨

Postural Reset

Targeted thoracic extension and scapular stabilization reverse chronic kyphotic distortion caused by screen time and prolonged seated commutes.

♩

Deep REM Sleep

Systemic somatic release reduces bedtime sympathetic spike, accelerating the onset of stage 3 slow-wave sleep and physical tissue repair.
