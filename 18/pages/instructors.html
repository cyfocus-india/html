Studio Ethics

Our Core Teaching Principles

We hold ourselves to rigorous standards of consent, anatomical safety, and cultural reverence.

✔

Consent-First Adjustments

Every student receives a double-sided indicator stone before practice (Green for adjustments welcome, Stone for silent space). Your physical boundaries are sacred and absolute.

⚖

Anatomical Individuality

No two hip sockets or femoral necks share the exact same skeletal angle. We teach to your unique bone structure rather than enforcing artificial aesthetic shapes.

♡

Trauma-Informed Space

Doors remain unlatched, low lighting is never pitch dark, and students are consistently offered agency to rest in Balasana (Child’s Pose) without judgment or unwanted attention.
