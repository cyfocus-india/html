Our Journey

From One Room to a Neighborhood Sanctuary

Aura grew slowly and on purpose: one teacher, one practice room, and a promise never to rush a student. Every expansion since has come from listening to the people on the mats.

  1. 2018

    The First Room

    Founder Maya Chen opens Aura above a tea shop on Blossom Alley with nine weekly classes, borrowed bolsters, and a single kettle.

  2. 2019

    A Home on Lotus Blossom Way

    We move into Suite 200 at 428 Lotus Blossom Way, lay our radiant heated bamboo floors, and grow to a team of six teachers.

  3. 2020

    Teacher Training Begins

    Our first Yoga Alliance 200-hour cohort graduates. Many of those teachers still lead classes on our schedule today.

  4. 2022

    The Sanctuary Loft Opens

    A dedicated loft for yin, yoga nidra, and crystal bowl ceremonies, lined with cedar acoustic panels for deep quiet.

  5. 2024

    The Recovery Suite

    Infrared cedar saunas, rainfall showers, and the tea library arrive, so the practice can continue long after savasana.

  6. 2026 • Today

    A Daily Anchor for 2,000+ Members

    38+ weekly classes, 12 resident teachers, and a community that keeps the same spirit as that first sunlit room.

How It Works

Your Path at Aura, Step by Step

No contracts to decode and no guesswork at the front desk. Here is exactly how practicing with us works, from your first booking to your hundredth class.

01

Choose Your Pass

Start with the 14-day Intro Pass for $49, drop in for $28, or choose a monthly membership. Every option includes mats, props, and tea.

02

Reserve Your Mat

Book any class from your member dashboard. When a class is full, join the waitlist and we will text you the moment a mat opens.

03

Arrive & Settle

Doors open 20 minutes early. Check in, collect your props, and pick a green or stone card to share how you feel about hands-on adjustments.

04

Practice & Grow

After your first month, book a complimentary check-in with a teacher to shape your weekly rhythm. Workshops and training await when you are ready.

The Ambiance

A Studio Designed for Every Sense

Ambiance is not decoration here. Light, sound, scent, air, and warmth are tuned with the same care as a sequence, so your body can settle before the first breath cue.

  • Soft, Circadian Light

    Tall north-facing windows soften the daylight. After sunset, warm 2700K dimmable lamps take over. Never harsh, and never pitch dark.

  • Quiet by Design

    Cedar acoustic slats keep street noise outside. Classes move to live singing bowls, low-tempo playlists, or simple, spacious silence.

  • Gentle, Natural Scent

    Practice rooms stay fragrance-free for sensitive noses. A light cedar and sandalwood diffuser welcomes you in the lounge only.

  • Grounding Warmth

    Radiant bamboo floors hold between 72°F and 78°F, matched to each class style, with organic cotton blankets for savasana.

  • Fresh, Filtered Air

    HEPA filtration refreshes every room every 8 minutes, so even a full vinyasa class feels light and clear.

  • A Ritual to Close

    Tulsi, chamomile, ginger, and lavender teas are brewed fresh daily, a quiet landing between your mat and the rest of your day.
