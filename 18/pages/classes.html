Progression Roadmap

Understanding Our Practice Levels

We honor individual anatomical variation. There is no hierarchy in yoga; choosing the appropriate level preserves joint longevity and creates sustainable progress.

Level 1 • Foundations

For newcomers or those resetting postural alignment.

Emphasis on joint positioning, pelvic tilts, foundational standing poses, and proper breathing mechanics. Teachers provide abundant verbal and visual cues.

  • ✓ Pace: Measured & steady
  • ✓ Prerequisite: None required
  • ✓ Ideal: Yin, Hatha, Restorative
Level 2 • Expansion

For practitioners with 6+ months consistent practice.

Introduces arm balances, gentle inversions (headstand and shoulderstand preps), deeper backbends, and longer flow sequences linked by ujjayi breath.

  • ✓ Pace: Fluid & rhythmic
  • ✓ Prerequisite: Comfortable with Sun Salutations
  • ✓ Ideal: Vinyasa Flow, Ashtanga Half
Level 3 • Deepening

For seasoned students and teachers seeking mastery.

Rigorous physical challenges, complex transitions (floating jump-throughs, pincha mayurasana), full bandha control, and extended kumbhaka breath retention.

  • ✓ Pace: Autonomous & vigorous
  • ✓ Prerequisite: Established personal practice
  • ✓ Ideal: Ashtanga Full Primary, Solar Flow
