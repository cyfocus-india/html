Helpful Knowledge

Studio Arrival Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to ensure your arrival is effortless and serene.

Wear breathable, flexible athletic attire. We practice barefoot on bamboo floors. You do not need to bring a mat—complimentary sanitized Manduka Pro mats, blocks, and straps are provided. Bring a reusable water bottle; we have chilled filtered alkaline water taps.

To preserve the quiet contemplative space and avoid disturbing centering breathwork, studio doors lock exactly 2 minutes before class. If you arrive late, you are welcome to enjoy the tea lounge and wait for the next scheduled session without losing your pass credit.

Yes. We have six private rain shower suites equipped with organic botanical shampoos, conditioners, body washes, and Dyson hair dryers. Fresh organic cotton towels are provided complimentary for all members.
