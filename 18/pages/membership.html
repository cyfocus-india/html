Membership Policies & Terms

We believe yoga studio policies should reflect peace of mind, not financial entrapment.

Members on monthly plans can freeze their subscription anytime directly from their member dashboard or by notifying reception. Freezing is available in increments of 7 days up to a cumulative 45 days per calendar year with zero pause fees.

None. We charge zero initiation fees and zero cancellation fees. You may cancel your monthly autopay at any time with a simple 14-day advance notice before your next billing cycle.

Yes. Class packs can be shared with one designated partner or family member. Simply notify our front desk coordinator and we will link your accounts in our booking system.

Because studio spots are capped to ensure physical spaciousness, we ask that you cancel at least 3 hours before class starts. Late cancels on unlimited plans incur a modest $10 fee donated directly to our community fund.
