First-Time Visitors

Your First Visit: Simple & Stress-Free

We know walking into a new studio can feel intimidating. Here is exactly what happens from the moment you step through our doors.

01

Warm Welcome

Arrive 15 minutes before class. Slip off your footwear at our cedar shoe bank, check in at the reception, and receive your locker key.

02

Complimentary Props

Grab a freshly sanitized Manduka PRO mat, organic cork blocks, and a cotton strap from our prop alcove—all provided free of charge.

03

Uncluttered Practice

Find your spot in the studio. Our teachers offer hands-on adjustments only with your explicit verbal permission and comfort level.

04

Herbal Integration

After savasana, linger in our herbal tea lounge. Savor an organic botanical tea, hydrate, and transition gently back into your day.
