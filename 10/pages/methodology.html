Implementation blueprint

The 5-stage framework in operational detail.

Stage 01

Assess & Diagnose

Every transformation begins with precision diagnosis to ensure training directly targets root-cause performance bottlenecks.

Timeline: Weeks 1 – 2

Core Activities

  • 360-degree competency audits and surveys
  • Executive sponsor & manager alignment interviews
  • Review of existing KPIs, toolchains, and workflows

Key Deliverables

  • Organizational Skill Gap Matrix & Heatmap
  • Cohort Baseline Capability Diagnostic Report
  • Agreed Success Metrics & Target ROI KPIs
Stage 02

Design & Contextualize

Off-the-shelf training fails because it lacks business relevance. We tailor each program around your actual work materials.

Timeline: Weeks 2 – 3

Core Activities

  • Co-creating realistic roleplay cases from real challenges
  • Calibrating difficulty levels to participant seniority
  • Designing interactive asynchronous pre-work modules

Key Deliverables

  • Bespoke Cohort Syllabus & Learning Journey Map
  • Scenario Simulation Playbooks & Toolkits
  • People Leader Coaching & Support Guide
Stage 03

Deliver & Facilitate

Dynamic, practitioner-led sessions where lecture takes a back seat to experiential simulations and deliberate practice.

Timeline: Weeks 3 – 6

Core Activities

  • Live scenario roleplays with real-time feedback
  • Small-group breakout problem-solving sprints
  • Individual reflection and personalized action planning

Key Deliverables

  • Live Session Attendance & Engagement Metrics
  • Formative Diagnostic Quizzes & Self-Assessments
  • Immediate Learner Reaction (Kirkpatrick Level 1)
Stage 04

Apply & Embed

True learning happens on the job. We support participants through structured workplace application projects and micro-nudges.

Timeline: Weeks 6 – 12

Core Activities

  • Weekly spaced micro-challenges delivered via email/portal
  • 1-on-1 or group coaching checkpoint sessions
  • Manager-observation behavioral milestone reviews

Key Deliverables

  • Applied Workplace Capstone Deliverable
  • 30-Day & 60-Day Habit Transfer Audits
  • Verified Manager Behavioral Sign-Off (Level 3)
Stage 05

Measure & Scale

We provide enterprise people leaders with definitive data demonstrating program ROI, cohort growth, and behavioral shifts.

Timeline: Post-Cohort / Quarterly

Core Activities

  • Aggregating completion, attendance, and exam data
  • Business impact correlation against company OKRs
  • Generating verified digital credentials with QR codes

Key Deliverables

  • Executive ROI & Capability Transformation Report
  • Verifiable Digital Completion Certificates
  • Continuous Upskilling Recommendations

Clarifications

Frequently asked questions about our pedagogy and approach.

How does Elevate Learning differ from standard e-learning or webinar providers?
Standard e-learning suffers from an average 85% drop-off rate because it is passive and disconnected from real work. Our methodology pairs live practitioner facilitation with customized workplace simulations, 90-day retention nudges, and Kirkpatrick Level 3/4 ROI measurement tracked directly through your HR portal.
How much customization is included in an enterprise program?
Every program goes through Stage 01 (Assess) and Stage 02 (Design). We weave your organization's specific terminology, customer challenges, software tools, and communication policies into every workshop exercise so learning looks and feels identical to actual work.
Can we export completion records and attendance to our company LMS?
Yes. Our learning management system is fully SCORM 1.2/2004 and xAPI compliant. HR administrators can download automated CSV reports, sync via webhooks, or embed module tracking into Workday, Cornerstone, SAP SuccessFactors, and custom internal portals.
How are trainers and facilitators selected for our specific cohort?
We match facilitators based on specific industry sector, operational domain experience, and participant seniority. All our facilitators have at least 12 years of executive leadership experience and undergo rigorous facilitation training.
