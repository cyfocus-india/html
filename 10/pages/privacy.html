Information we collect

We collect information you provide through enquiries, account registration, training enrollment and portal use. This can include your name, work contact details, organization, role, program participation, attendance and completion data.

How we use information

We use this information to deliver and administer learning programs, provide reports and certificates, respond to enquiries, maintain portal security and improve our services.

When information is shared

Information may be shared with authorized administrators at your organization, contracted facilitators and service providers where necessary to deliver the program. We do not sell personal information to any third parties.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete applicable personal information, subject to legal and contractual retention requirements. Your organization may also control training records managed through its HR manager account.

Security and retention

We use appropriate organizational and technical safeguards to protect your data against unauthorized access. Records are retained only as long as needed for the purpose collected, contractual obligations and applicable compliance standards.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact our privacy team at privacy@elevatelearning.example.
