Implementation Roadmap

Custom academy co-design & milestone timeline.

A rigorous 4-phase rollout ensuring curriculum relevance, manager buy-in, and measurable capability transfer.

Phase 01 · Week 1

Diagnostics & Gap Audit

Frontline stakeholder interviews, workflow observation, and skill taxonomy mapping to isolate urgent performance bottlenecks.

Phase 02 · Week 2–3

Contextualized Co-Design

Authoring authentic workplace simulation scenarios, manager coaching rubrics, and custom organizational case studies.

Phase 03 · Week 4

Pilot Cohort & Calibration

Alpha test run with key team champions to calibrate pacing, gather immediate learner feedback, and fine-tune exercises.

Phase 04 · Week 5+

Enterprise Rollout & ROI

Staggered multi-department cohort delivery, automated LMS tracking, and manager habit reinforcement nudges.
