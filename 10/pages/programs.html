Clarifications & Logistics

Frequently Asked Questions About Our Programs

Quick answers to key operational, scheduling, and credential questions asked by learners and L&D managers.

How are live cohort sessions scheduled for distributed global teams?
We offer dual timezone scheduling tracks (APAC/EMEA and EMEA/Americas). All live sessions are recorded in high-definition and accompanied by interactive sandbox catch-up exercises for learners with unexpected client obligations.
Do our employees earn Continuing Education Units (CEU) or CPE credits?
Yes. All Elevate Learning programs are aligned with ANSI/IACET Continuing Education Unit standards. Certified graduates receive official transcripts denoting completed instructional contact hours and CEUs.
Can managers monitor cohort attendance and assessment scores in real time?
Authorized HR managers and department leads receive dedicated dashboard access with live metrics tracking individual attendance, module quiz completion, capstone status, and weekly practice application data.
What is the prerequisite criteria for specialized vs foundational programs?
Foundational programs have zero technical prerequisites. For intermediate and executive tracks, a complimentary 15-minute diagnostic self-audit helps determine whether the learner will get maximum value from the cohort depth.
