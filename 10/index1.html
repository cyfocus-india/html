Common questions

Frequently asked questions by L&D leaders.

How does Elevate Learning measure actual on-the-job capability transfer?
We employ the Kirkpatrick 4-Level Evaluation Model. Beyond immediate learner reaction surveys, we implement post-training micro-projects, manager observation scorecards at 30 and 60 days, and correlate completion data with your team's departmental KPIs.
Can programs be integrated with our existing LMS and HR portal?
Yes. Our learning management infrastructure supports standard SCORM 1.2/2004 and xAPI integrations, and our HR portal provides secure CSV, REST API, and single sign-on (SSO) options to sync attendance and certifications seamlessly.
What is the typical time commitment required from our employees?
Our enterprise cohorts are calibrated for busy professionals: typically two 90-minute live interactive sessions per week over 3 to 4 weeks, paired with 20 minutes of weekly asynchronous micro-practice directly tied to their daily workflow.
Who facilitates the programs and what are their qualifications?
All Elevate Learning facilitators are vetted practitioners with a minimum of 12 years of real-world operational leadership in top-tier organizations. They bring practical field insight, not theoretical academic lectures.
