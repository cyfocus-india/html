Severity Protocol

Emergency Severity Classifications

We prioritize life safety, structural preservation, and commercial continuity. Here is how we triage incoming distress calls.

Priority 1 • Extreme

Burst Pipe & Active Flooding

High-velocity water escaping from fractured copper, frozen PEX joints, or ruptured toilet supply valves. Immediate threat to structural ceilings and floor joists.

Target Dispatch: Under 30 Mins
Priority 2 • Biohazard

Mainline Sewage Backflow

Blackwater surging backward through floor drains, tubs, or lower-level toilets. Contains toxic bacteria and pathogens requiring high-pressure jetting.

Target Dispatch: Under 35 Mins
Priority 3 • Hazard

Water Heater Tank Failure

Tank seam splitting, temperature-pressure relief valve spewing boiling water, or internal corrosive rupture. Poses scalding and structural water damage risk.

Target Dispatch: Under 45 Mins
Priority 4 • Commercial

Commercial Kitchen Shutdown

Backed-up restaurant grease traps, failed booster pumps in office towers, or complete municipal supply failure halting revenue operations.

Target Dispatch: Under 40 Mins
