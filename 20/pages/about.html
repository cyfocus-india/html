Our Story

From One Van to the Metro's Most Trusted Plumbing Crew

HydroFix began in 2004 when master plumber Marcus Reyes left a large franchise outfit, tired of watching customers get upsold on repairs they didn't need. He started with a single van, a pipe wrench, and one simple rule: quote the job in writing before touching a single pipe.

Two decades later we run four dispatch depots and a fleet of fully stocked mobile workshops, yet that rule still sits at the heart of every call. Every technician is a licensed HydroFix employee, never a subcontractor, trained in-house to the standards Marcus set on day one.

"A plumber's real job is protecting people's homes. The pipe is just where the work happens."

Marcus Reyes, Founder & Master Plumber
  1. 2004

    Founded With a Single Service Van

    Marcus Reyes opens HydroFix in a rented garage, offering written flat-rate quotes when open-ended hourly billing was the norm.

  2. 2009

    24/7 Emergency Dispatch Launches

    A dedicated night crew and round-the-clock hotline bring our average emergency arrival time under 45 minutes.

  3. 2014

    Commercial Division Opens

    Restaurants, clinics, and office towers join our client base with backflow testing and grease interceptor programs.

  4. 2019

    Zero-Destruction Diagnostics

    Acoustic leak sensors, 4K sewer cameras, and thermal imagers let our crews find hidden leaks without opening walls.

  5. 2026

    14 Vans, Four Depots, One Promise

    GPS-routed vans now cover the entire metro area from four depots, and every job is still quoted in writing first.
