Before they teach your child

Every tutor teaches a live demo class first.

Qualifications get an applicant to the interview. What decides it is a 30-minute class taught to real students, watched by our academic directors.

  • A written subject paper set at one class level above what they will teach.
  • A live demo class, scored on clarity, patience and how they handle a wrong answer.
  • Police verification and reference checks with two previous schools.
  • A classroom observation every term, with feedback shared openly.
