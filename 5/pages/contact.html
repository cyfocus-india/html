Quick answers

Common Admissions Questions

Frequently asked questions about enrolments, timings, and trial classes.

Can we attend a trial demo class?

Yes! We offer a complimentary, no-obligation trial class in any subject so you and your child can experience our small-batch teaching style firsthand.

Can students join mid-term?

Absolutely. Our tutors conduct an initial bridge assessment and run tailored catch-up sessions to quickly bring new students up to speed with the batch.

What if a student misses a class?

All sessions include structured lesson summaries, and students can attend our open weekend doubt clinics to cover any missed concepts.
