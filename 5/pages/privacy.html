About this preview

This Northstar Tutoring website is a static demonstration template. The student names, results, faculty profiles and testimonials are illustrative. It does not operate a live tutoring service or account system.

Information entered in forms

Contact, registration and notification forms validate entries in your browser. They do not send submissions to a server. Passwords and enquiry details are not stored. Please use the supplied demo credentials to explore the dashboard.

Local preferences

The site stores your theme and reading-direction preferences in your browser’s local storage. These settings remain on your device and can be cleared through your browser settings. No analytics or advertising cookies are included.

Students and guardians

This template is intended for a school tutoring centre. A production version should provide appropriate guardian involvement and a reviewed privacy policy before collecting any student information.

Images and resources

The site includes locally stored fonts, icons and AI-generated images of fictional people. Fonts and icons are distributed under their respective open-source licences.

Before a public launch

The operator should replace this preview notice with an accurate policy describing its own data collection, retention, security, contact details and service providers. This template notice is not a substitute for that review.
