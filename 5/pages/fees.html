A few helpful answers

Before you enrol

Can I change my subjects later?

Yes. Talk to your academic advisor before the next billing month so your plan and batch can be updated.

Are the study materials charged separately?

No. The displayed plans include tutor-shared notes, worksheets and regular practice assessments.

Can I try a class before enrolling?

You can request an introductory class through the enquiry form. The centre will confirm the next available slot.

When are classes held?

Weekday classes run after school, with additional Saturday batches. Exact timings depend on your grade and subjects.
