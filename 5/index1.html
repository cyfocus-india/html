Inside a session

What one hour actually looks like

No lectures into the void. Every class follows the same rhythm, so students always know what is coming next.

  • First 10 minutes — last class recap and a quick three-question warm-up.
  • Next 20 minutes — the new concept, worked on the board with the class solving alongside.
  • Next 20 minutes — independent practice while the tutor moves desk to desk.
  • Last 10 minutes — doubts cleared out loud, homework set, and a note home if anything needs attention.
