Mathematics — Triangles Practice
Class 10 | Tutor: Rohit Verma | 5 September 2026

Two triangles are similar when their corresponding angles are equal and corresponding sides are proportional.
Similarity criteria include AA, SSS and SAS (with the included angle equal).
For similar triangles, the ratio of areas equals the square of the ratio of corresponding sides.

Practice:
1. Similar triangles have corresponding sides in the ratio 2:3. Find their area ratio.
2. A right triangle has legs 6 cm and 8 cm. Find its hypotenuse.
3. A triangle with sides 3, 4 and 5 cm is similar to one whose shortest side is 9 cm. Find its other sides.
4. State what information is needed to apply SAS similarity.

Answers:
1. 4:9. 2. 10 cm. 3. 12 cm and 15 cm.
4. Two pairs of corresponding sides are proportional and their included angles are equal.

Always label corresponding vertices in the same order.
