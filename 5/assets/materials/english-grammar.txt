NORTHSTAR TUTORING — FOR SCHOOL STUDENTS
English — Grammar Practice
Class 10 | Tutor: Kavita Sharma | 8 September 2026

Focus: subject–verb agreement, tense consistency and clear sentences.

Choose the correct verb:
1. Each of the students (has / have) a notebook.
2. The books on the desk (belongs / belong) to Ananya.
3. Neither the teacher nor the students (was / were) late.

Rewrite in reported speech:
4. Riya said, "I am reading a novel."
5. The tutor said to us, "Please check your answers."

Punctuate:
6. after the lesson aarav asked can we try another example

Answer guide:
1. has. 2. belong. 3. were.
4. Riya said that she was reading a novel.
5. The tutor asked us to check our answers.
6. After the lesson, Aarav asked, "Can we try another example?"

Writing task: Describe a moment when asking a question helped you learn. Use 100–120 words and check every sentence for clarity.
