Science — Life Processes
Class 10 | Tutor: Priya Menon | 9 September 2026

Living organisms carry out processes that maintain life. Core examples include nutrition, respiration, transport and excretion.

Nutrition: Green plants make carbohydrates using carbon dioxide and water with light energy and chlorophyll. Animals obtain food from other organisms.
Respiration: Cells release usable energy from food. Aerobic respiration uses oxygen; anaerobic pathways do not.
Transport: Blood carries oxygen, nutrients and wastes in humans. Xylem carries water and minerals in plants; phloem transports sugars.
Excretion: Kidneys remove nitrogenous wastes and regulate water and salts in humans.

Practice:
1. Explain why digestion and respiration are different processes.
2. Compare xylem and phloem.
3. State the role of alveoli in gas exchange.

Answer guide:
1. Digestion breaks large food molecules into absorbable forms; cellular respiration releases energy from food molecules.
2. Xylem transports water/minerals; phloem transports sugars to where they are used or stored.
3. Alveoli provide a large, thin, moist surface with a blood supply for diffusion.
