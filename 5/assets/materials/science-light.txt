Science — Light: Reflection & Refraction
Class 10 | Tutor: Priya Menon | 3 September 2026

Reflection is the return of light into the same medium after striking a surface. The angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection, measured from the normal.

Refraction is the change in direction when light crosses between media at an angle because its speed changes. Light entering a medium with a higher refractive index bends towards the normal.

Practice:
1. A ray strikes a mirror at 35 degrees to the normal. Find the angle of reflection.
2. What happens to a ray that enters glass from air along the normal?
3. Define refractive index relative to vacuum.
4. Draw a labelled ray diagram for refraction from air into glass.

Answers:
1. 35 degrees. 2. Its speed changes, but its direction does not.
3. n = c/v, where c is the speed of light in vacuum and v is the speed in the medium.
4. Include incident ray, refracted ray, surface and normal. The refracted ray bends towards the normal.

Check sign conventions in your textbook before using mirror or lens formulas.
