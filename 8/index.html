Never played modern board games before? You don’t have to read thick rulebooks or bring a full group—we’ve made sitting down to play as easy as coffee.

Our friendly Game Gurus set up the board and teach everyone the rules in under 5 minutes so you start having fun immediately.

Enjoy local craft coffee and snacks while playing. Loved the game? Take home a fresh copy with 10% off your table tab.
