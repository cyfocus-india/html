Privacy Policy

Information entered into forms

Contact, RSVP, sign-in, and sign-up forms demonstrate client-side validation only. Submitted values are cleared after the confirmation message and are not sent anywhere.

Local preferences

The site stores your light/dark theme and left-to-right/right-to-left layout preferences in your browser’s local storage. These values contain no personal information and can be cleared through your browser settings.

Analytics and cookies

This template includes no analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies. If you add third-party services, update this policy before publishing.

Your choices

You can use the site without entering personal details. For questions about a production implementation, contact hello@meepleandmarrow.example.
