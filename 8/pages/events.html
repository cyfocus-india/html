The Welcoming Table Pledge.

Whether you’re a first-time guest or a Friday night regular, here’s how we keep our tables warm, inclusive, and fun for everyone.

Fun Over Winning

We play with spirit, but we laugh harder. Gracious winners and proud runners-up make the best table memories.

Zero-Homework Guarantee

Never worry about reading manuals before arriving. Our hosts explain the rules round-by-round so you start playing immediately.

No Gatekeeping, Ever

Seasoned grandmasters and first-timers share the same tables with mutual patience, generosity, and excitement.

Treat Games With Care

Clean hands, drinks in table cup-holders, and gentle shuffling keep our 450+ demo library in mint condition for everyone.

Solo Players Welcome

Arriving alone? You won’t be alone for long.

Over 40% of our evening attendees walk in solo. We’ve made joining a game table completely effortless and anxiety-free.

Step 01

Greet & Pick Your Vibe

Walk in and tell our greeting host what energy you’re feeling—fast laughs, deep strategy, or relaxed tile placement.

Step 02

Meet Your Table Crew

We introduce you to friendly players with open seats, make casual introductions, and get everyone comfortable before unboxing.

Step 03

Start Playing in 5 Mins

A dedicated table host sets up the components and kicks off the teach so you start rolling dice and laughing without awkward delays.
