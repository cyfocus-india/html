Our story & mission

A game store should feel like an invitation.

Meeple & Marrow began with a simple belief: a thoughtful recommendation can turn an ordinary evening into a tradition. Today we offer over 450+ playable titles, open table gaming, weekly events, and warm hospitality in Bengaluru.

Explore our shelves Join a game night
“The best game on the shelf is the one your table asks to play again.”

We opened our doors in 2014 with three shelves, one long wooden table, and a handwritten list of games we could teach without opening a rulebook. The shelves grew to hold more than 450 international and indie titles. The welcoming table stayed.

Today we carry modern strategy games, family favourites, quick-play card games, tactile wooden puzzles, and tabletop role-playing accessories. But our work is still deeply personal. We play what we sell. We ask who is coming over. And we tell you honestly if a game is not the right fit for your group.

That honesty is how a shop becomes a community: one clear teach, one welcoming seat, and one memorable evening at a time.

Our Story in Chapters

2014

Three Shelves & A Dream

What guides us

More table. Less noise.

Every choice we make—from which titles earn shelf space to how we run Friday night game socials—is rooted in four principles.

Curiosity over hype

We choose games for the genuine tabletop experiences they create, not the marketing noise around them. Every title has earned its place through play.

Welcome is a practice

Clear teaching, accessible events, and patient recommendations are part of our product. There is always room for one more chair at our tables.

Honest guidance

If a game will not fit your player count, attention span, or mood, we will gladly help you find one that will.

The Meeple & Marrow Standard

Four things you’ll never find on our shelves.

We’re not an algorithm or an impersonal warehouse. Here is our personal promise to every player who walks through our doors.

Not Here

No Unvetted Hype

We never stock games just because they trended online. Every box is unboxed, played, and debated by our crew first.

Not Here

No Rulebook Panic

If a game cannot be clearly explained in five minutes, we won’t recommend it as your table starter.

Not Here

No Dust Collectors

Not Here

No Rush to Leave

When you pull up a chair at our tables, stay as long as the games and conversation flow. You belong here.
