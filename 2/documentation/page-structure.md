# Page Structure

- `index.html`: Primary public landing page with hero, service advantages, performance statistics, testimonials, call to action, and footer.
- `index1.html`: Alternate home page focused on the four-step learning journey and student portal.
- `pages/courses.html`: Two-wheeler, four-wheeler, and refresher course cards.
- `pages/instructors.html`: Instructor profiles, specialties, ratings, and booking actions.
- `pages/vehicles.html`: Manual, automatic, and two-wheeler fleet information.
- `pages/fees.html`: Transparent pricing plans and payment information.
- `pages/dashboard.html`: Lesson booking, progress tracking, instructor feedback, and printable progress report.
- `pages/profile.html`: Editable student-profile demo.
- `pages/signin.html` and `pages/signup.html`: Validated account forms.
- `pages/about.html` and `pages/contact.html`: Supporting business and contact pages.
- `pages/privacy.html` and `pages/terms.html`: Policy pages.
- `pages/404.html` and `pages/coming-soon.html`: Utility pages.

Shared public-page UI is assembled by `assets/js/main.js`. The dashboard is intentionally self-contained so its navigation and app layout can differ from the public site.
