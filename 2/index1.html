Maneuver 01 • Steering Rhythm

The '8' Track (Figure Eight)

Continuous hand-over-hand steering rhythm and micro-throttle adjustments without foot-tapping, stopping, or touching electronic boundary poles.

Instructor Pro-Tip Keep your gaze locked on the apex of the curve, not your front bumper. Let the wheel glide back smoothly through your palms.
Time Limit: 120s Touch Penalty: Fail

Maneuver 02 • Spatial Precision

The 'H' Track (Forward & Reverse)

Drive cleanly into the left bay, change into reverse gear, and transition across the narrow perpendicular chute into the opposing bay with zero tyre scrubs.

Instructor Pro-Tip Use our C-pillar reference marker: begin counter-steering the instant your rear axle clears the bay entry cone.
Max Shifts: 2 Kerb Clearance: ≥ 20cm

Maneuver 03 • Parking Mastery

3-Move Parallel Kerb Parking

Reverse-slide smoothly between simulated vehicles into a 5.5-metre bay in exactly three fluid moves, finishing parallel within 15 cm of the kerb.

Instructor Pro-Tip Lock full lock at 45° until your passenger mirror aligns with the rear corner flag, then straighten and tuck in cleanly.

Maneuver 04 • Clutch Equilibrium

30° Gradient Hill Start & Stop

Stop completely on a steep upward incline, hold with handbrake, and start off smoothly without rolling backward even a single inch or stalling the engine.

Instructor Pro-Tip Feel for the steering vibration and engine pitch change at clutch bite, dial in light throttle, and release handbrake smoothly.
Rollback: 0 cm Cut-off: 3s

Maneuver 05 • Mirror Navigation

The Reverse 'S' Bend Curve

Navigate a narrow serpentine reverse course using wing mirrors and slow clutch control without touching safety boundary bollards.

Instructor Pro-Tip Alternate your wing mirror scans every 2 seconds. Small micro-adjustments prevent the rear end from swinging too wide.
Track Width: 2.8m Forward Stops: 0

Maneuver 06 • Reaction & Control

Emergency Hazard Braking

Accelerate to 30 km/h and execute an instantaneous, straight-line emergency threshold stop upon encountering a sudden obstacle signal.

Instructor Pro-Tip Stomp the brake firmly and simultaneously press the clutch to prevent stalling. Maintain firm two-handed steering center.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your driving lessons.

How many driving lessons do I need to get my license?
Most beginner drivers need between 15 to 20 one-hour practical lessons to gain full confidence and clear the RTO driving test on their first attempt. Our Four-Wheeler Comprehensive package includes 20 structured sessions covering road basics, city traffic, parking drills, and two mock tests.
Do I need a Learner's License before booking lessons?
Yes, in accordance with transport regulations, you must possess a valid Learner's License (LL) to drive on public roads. If you do not have one yet, our front desk team provides full assistance with LL online application and RTO slot booking.
Can I choose between Manual and Automatic transmission?
Absolutely! We have dedicated fleets for both manual and automatic training. You can choose your preferred transmission during enrollment or switch anytime depending on your learning goals.
What is your lesson rescheduling policy?
We understand schedules change. You can easily reschedule or cancel an upcoming lesson through the online student dashboard up to 12 hours before your scheduled time slot at no extra charge.
Do you conduct mock driving tests before the official RTO exam?
Yes! All our comprehensive courses include 2 realistic mock driving tests graded on the exact parameters checked by RTO examiners (reverse S-bend, 8-track, parallel parking, and hill-start clutch control).
