Inside a Session

What a 60-Minute Lesson Looks Like

Every practical session follows the same four-part structure, so you always know what you are working on and how it will be assessed.

  1. First 5 minutes

    Briefing & Objective

    Your instructor names the single skill for the session and shows you what "good" looks like before you move off.

  2. Next 15 minutes

    Controlled Drill

    The skill is isolated in a quiet lot or on our practice track, repeated until the movement stops feeling deliberate.

  3. Next 30 minutes

    Live Road Application

    The same skill is applied in real traffic on a route chosen to test it, with commentary coaching rather than interruption.

  4. Final 10 minutes

    Debrief & Written Feedback

    You review what improved, what to practise, and the notes are published to your dashboard before you get home.

Before You Enrol

Eligibility & Documents

Indian transport rules set clear minimums before you can take lessons on public roads. Check these first — if anything is missing, our front desk handles the applications for you at no extra charge.

  • Age: 16+ for gearless two-wheelers (under 50cc), 18+ for geared two-wheelers and all cars
  • Learner's Licence: a valid LL is mandatory before any on-road session
  • Identity proof: Aadhaar, passport, voter ID or PAN card
  • Address proof: utility bill, rental agreement or Aadhaar
  • Photographs: three recent passport-size photos
  • Medical: Form 1 self-declaration, or Form 1A from a registered doctor if you are over 40

Course Questions

Before You Choose a Program

The questions our advisors are asked most often at enrolment.

Can I switch courses after I have started?
Yes. Sessions already completed are credited at their per-lesson value against the new package, and you pay only the difference. The most common switch is from Four-Wheeler to Intensive when a test date moves forward, and it can be done at any point before your final mock test.
What happens if I need more lessons than the package includes?
Additional sessions are charged at a flat ₹450 each with no minimum block, and your instructor will tell you honestly whether you need them. Roughly one learner in five takes two or three extra lessons; nobody is pushed into a larger package they will not use.
Do lessons have to be on consecutive days?
Only on the Intensive Fast-Track, where the daily rhythm is the point. Every other course is scheduled entirely around you — twice a week, weekends only, or a burst before a test date. We do recommend no more than a ten-day gap between sessions, since longer breaks mean re-covering ground.
Is the same instructor assigned for the whole course?
Yes, you keep one primary instructor so nobody has to re-learn your habits. A second coach steps in only for a specialist module — highway work or a mock test with a different assessor — and for planned leave, where you are told in advance.
Can I use my own car for lessons?
On the Refresher course, yes — many students specifically want practice in the car they will actually drive. Your vehicle needs valid insurance, a current pollution certificate, and it must pass a short safety check on the first session. Beginner courses require our dual-control cars for safety reasons.
What if I fail the RTO driving test?
We book you two free refresher sessions targeted at whatever the examiner marked, plus another mock test before your re-attempt. You pay only the RTO's own re-test fee. Across all courses, 92% of our students pass first time and the large majority of the rest clear it on the second attempt.
