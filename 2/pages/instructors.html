Our Standards

How We Select Instructors

Roughly one in nine applicants makes it onto the team. Here is what everyone has to clear.

Background Verification

Police clearance, address verification and a clean traffic-violation record covering the previous five years.

Licence & Experience Floor

A valid instructor endorsement plus a minimum of five years of active commercial driving before they teach anyone.

Teaching Assessment

A scored mock lesson judged on patience, clarity of instruction and how faults are corrected without intimidation.

Annual Re-Certification

Every instructor re-sits a driving assessment and a first-aid module each year, with student ratings reviewed quarterly.

How We Teach

Correction Without Intimidation

Most people who are afraid of driving are not afraid of the car — they are afraid of being shouted at while operating it. Our instructors are trained to correct faults the way a good coach does: name the error, explain the consequence, then give you the repetition to fix it.

  • Commentary coaching. Your instructor narrates hazards aloud early on, then hands that job to you as your scanning improves.
  • One skill per session. Lessons have a single named objective, so progress is visible rather than vague.
  • No raised voices. The dual controls exist precisely so nobody has to panic; instructors intervene with the pedals, not volume.
  • Written feedback every time. Notes are published to your dashboard, so you can see the pattern across weeks.
  • You set the pace. Nobody is taken onto a busy road before they say they are ready for it.
