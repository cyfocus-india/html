Cancellations

Refund & Rescheduling Policy

Written out in full, because this is where most driving schools stay deliberately vague.

Rescheduling a lesson

Free of charge up to 12 hours before the slot, through the dashboard or by phone. Inside 12 hours the session counts as used, unless it is a medical reason with a note.

Cancelling a course

Before the first lesson: full refund minus a ₹500 administrative fee. After lessons have started: a refund of unused sessions at the standard per-lesson rate, processed within 10 working days.

Cancellations by us

If we cancel for weather, vehicle failure or instructor illness, the session is rescheduled at your convenience and does not count against your package. No charge, ever.

Package validity is 6 months from enrolment (3 months for the Intensive Fast-Track). Extensions are granted free on request for medical or relocation reasons.

Billing Questions

Questions About Cost

What students ask our accounts desk most often.

Is the advertised price the final price?
Yes for everything we control. The package price covers lessons, fuel, vehicle maintenance, insurance, mock tests, protective gear and dashboard access — there is no registration fee, material fee or fuel surcharge added later. The only separate costs are the government RTO charges listed in the table above, which you pay directly to the RTO at their own rate.
Can I pay for lessons one at a time instead of a package?
You can, at ₹550 per single lesson rather than the ₹450 package rate. Most learners start with one or two single sessions to make sure the instructor suits them, then convert to a package — and we credit the full amount already paid against the package price when they do.
Do you charge more for automatic transmission?
No. Manual and automatic lessons are the same price, and you can switch between them mid-course without a fee. The only pricing difference across our fleet is the Commercial LMV package, which costs more because it covers vans and MUVs in addition to cars.
Is there a discount for students or groups?
Full-time students with a valid college ID get 10% off any package. Groups of three or more enrolling together — friends, family or colleagues — also get 10%, billed on a single consolidated invoice if a company is paying. The two discounts do not stack.
How does the two-part payment actually work?
You pay half at enrolment and the rest before your halfway lesson — lesson 10 on the Four-Wheeler package, lesson 8 on Commercial LMV, day 8 on the Intensive. There is no interest, no credit check and no processing fee, and if you stop mid-course you have only paid for what you have used.
Do I get a proper invoice for reimbursement?
Yes. A GST invoice is emailed within minutes of every payment and stored permanently in your student dashboard, so you can re-download it whenever an employer or insurer asks. Invoices can be raised in a company's name if you tell us at enrolment.
