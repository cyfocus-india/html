Institutional Accountability

The DriveWay Safety Charter & Student Bill of Rights

We hold our school, vehicles, and instructors to uncompromising ethical standards. Every learner who steps into our car is protected by six non-negotiable institutional guarantees.

Article I

The Calm Cockpit Guarantee

Driving anxiety is real. We strictly prohibit yelling, impatience, sarcasm, or harsh gestures. Learning happens through supportive, phased reinforcement.

Student Right: If an instructor raises their voice, your lesson is 100% refunded and re-scheduled with a senior mentor.
Article II

100% Behind-The-Wheel Time

A 60-minute booking is a full 60 minutes of active coaching. Instructors never use student lesson time for personal errands, phone calls, or fueling stops.

Student Right: Any lost training minutes are logged via your student portal and credited back immediately.
Article III

Dedicated One-on-One Attention

We never crowd vehicles with strangers or force you to wait in the back seat while another pupil drives during your booked individual slot.

Student Right: You enjoy complete cabin privacy and undivided 1:1 instructor focus throughout every mile.
Article IV

Dual-Control Pre-Flight Inspection

Every vehicle undergoes an 18-point mechanical inspection every morning, certifying instructor dual pedals, tyre tread, brakes, and dashcam telemetry.

Student Right: You can view the live daily inspection sign-off certificate directly in your student app before driving.
Article V

No-Questions-Asked Instructor Switch

Learning chemistry matters. If you don't feel 100% comfortable with your assigned coach, you have the unilateral right to switch anytime.

Student Right: Request a female instructor or switch coaches via the web portal with zero awkward confrontations.
Article VI

Zero Student Financial Liability

All training vehicles are insured under our comprehensive commercial driving school policy with zero deductible required from learners.

Student Right: In the rare event of a parking scrape or curb contact, you carry zero personal repair liabilities.
