How a visit works

Four calm steps to fabulous

  1. Say hello

    A slow meet-and-greet, a sniff of the room and a treat. We note any sore spots or worries.

  2. Bath & pamper

    Warm water, pH-balanced shampoo matched to the coat and a gentle, low-noise dryer.

  3. Style & finish

    Breed-standard or custom trims, paw pads, nails and ears, all with plenty of breaks.

  4. Home, happy

    A photo update for you, a bandana for them, and grooming notes saved to your account.

Low-noise dryers only

Fear-free promise

Designed around how pets feel

  • Quiet, cage-free salon

    No barking rows of kennels. Pets wait with a groomer or in a calm, open suite.

  • Skin-first products

    Plant-based, fragrance-light shampoos from Wholesome Paws, matched to each coat.

  • Breaks whenever needed

    We pause, play and try again. If a pet is too stressed, we stop and plan a gentler visit.

  • Live photo updates

    A WhatsApp photo mid-groom and when they're ready, so you're never left wondering.
