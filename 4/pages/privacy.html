Privacy Policy

1. Information we collect

When you shop, book grooming or create an account we collect your name, contact details, delivery address and order history, plus pet details you choose to share such as name, breed, age and health notes. We also collect limited technical data (browser type, pages visited) to keep the site working well.

2. How we use it

We use your information to fulfil orders, confirm appointments, send the updates you ask for, manage Paw Points and improve our range. We never sell your personal data.

3. Your pets' information

Health and behaviour notes are shared only with the groomer or specialist caring for your pet, and are kept to make every visit safer and calmer.

4. Cookies & local storage

This site stores your theme, layout direction, basket and wishlist in your browser's local storage so they're remembered between visits. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings.

5. Sharing with partners

We share only what's needed with delivery partners and payment processors to complete your order. Each is bound by confidentiality obligations.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data at any time from your account settings or by emailing us. We'll respond within 30 days.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@wagandwhisker.example or write to us at Shop 7, Lane 5, North Main Road, Koregaon Park, Pune 411001.
