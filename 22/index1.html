Good to know

Questions travellers ask before booking

How much do I pay to confirm, and when is the balance due?

25% of the package price confirms your booking. The balance is due 21 days before departure for domestic trips and 30 days before for international trips. Bookings above ₹50,000 can use 0% EMI for 3 or 6 months on major credit cards.

What happens if I need to cancel?

Cancel 45+ days before departure and we refund everything except non-refundable flight fares and bank charges. Between 44 and 15 days, 50% of the land cost is refunded; inside 15 days the land cost is non-refundable. Insurance on international trips covers medical cancellations.

Can I change my itinerary after booking?

Yes. Changes up to 15 days before travel are free if the hotel and transport allow it; we only pass on any fare difference.

Are flights included?

International packages include return economy flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Kochi or Ahmedabad. Domestic packages are land-only so you can use miles or trains; we can add flights at cost.

Do you plan for seniors, kids or travellers with limited mobility?

Often. We pick hotels with lifts and ground-floor rooms, book wheelchair assistance at airports, keep daily drives under four hours and arrange Jain, vegetarian or child-friendly meals.
