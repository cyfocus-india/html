Our story

Started by two travellers who were tired of copy-paste tours

In 2011, Karthik Rao and Anjali Menon left a large tour operator with one idea: every trip should be planned by someone who has actually been there. They rented two desks above a bakery on 100 Feet Road and began with weekend road trips to Coorg, Kerala and Goa for Bengaluru families.

The desks have multiplied since, but the idea hasn't. Every Wanderkite planner has travelled to at least 15 of the places they sell, every partner hotel is inspected in person, and the person who plans your trip is the one who picks up when something goes wrong.

Milestones

From two desks to a four-city team

A few of the moments that shaped how we plan today.

  1. 2011

    Two desks on 100 Feet Road

    Wanderkite opens in Indiranagar with weekend road trips to Coorg, Kerala and Goa for Bengaluru families.

  2. 2014

    Our first international trips

    Bali and Thailand join the list, priced with flights, visas and insurance from day one — so the number you see is the number you pay.

  3. 2016

    IATA accreditation

    We start issuing air tickets in-house, so fares are locked the moment you confirm instead of days later.

  4. 2018

    Mumbai, Delhi and the rescue desk

    Walk-in offices open in Bandra and Connaught Place, and the 24×7 on-trip rescue desk goes live in Bengaluru.

  5. 2022

    Kochi office and the traveller dashboard

    Our fourth office opens on MG Road, and bookings, e-tickets and invoices move into one online dashboard.

  6. 2026

    30+ countries, 22 Indian states

    26 planners, 48,000+ travellers and the rule we started with: plan only what you'd happily take yourself.
