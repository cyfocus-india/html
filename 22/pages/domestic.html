Featured itinerary

Ladakh, day by day

Our most-booked summer trip is paced for altitude: two slow days in Leh before any high pass, and no day longer than seven hours on the road.

Day 1Land in Leh (3,500 m) and rest

Airport pick-up and a full afternoon of rest to acclimatise. Short evening walk to Shanti Stupa for sunset over the Indus valley.

🛏 Stay: Leh — heritage guesthouse, dinner included

Day 2Monasteries of the Indus valley

Morning prayers at Thiksey, then Hemis — Ladakh's richest monastery — and the ruins of Shey Palace. Back in Leh by 4 PM.

🛏 Stay: Leh

Day 3Over Khardung La to Nubra

Cross one of the world's highest motorable passes (5,359 m). Afternoon among the sand dunes of Hunder with an optional double-humped camel ride.

🛏 Stay: Hunder — desert camp with attached baths

Day 4Shyok river road to Pangong

A dramatic drive along the Shyok river to Pangong Tso, the blue lake that changes colour through the day. Bonfire dinner by the water.

🛏 Stay: Pangong — lakeside Swiss tents with heaters

Day 5Pangong sunrise, back via Chang La

Watch the sun rise over the lake, then return to Leh across Chang La (5,360 m). Free evening at Leh's main bazaar.

🛏 Stay: Leh

Day 6Sham valley and the Sangam

Magnetic Hill, Gurudwara Pathar Sahib and the confluence of the Indus and Zanskar rivers. Optional rafting on the Zanskar (₹1,800).

🛏 Stay: Leh

Day 7Fly home

Breakfast and transfer to Kushok Bakula Rimpochee Airport for your flight home.
